# Al-Azhar secondary curriculum: creed (tawhid)

Collected 2026-11-10 for `civ/chapters/03_ch03.md`, section 2.

## The books

*Taysir Sharh Jawharat al-Tawhid lil-Shaykh Ibrahim al-Bayjuri* (a simplified edition of al-Bajuri's commentary on al-Laqani's *Jawharat al-Tawhid*). Al-Azhar al-Sharif, Sector of Azhari Institutes (قطاع المعاهد الأزهرية), Committee for Preparing and Developing Curricula. Academic year 1440–1441 AH / 2019–2020. One volume each for the first, second, and third years of Azhari secondary school.

* The first-year PDF is from archive.org item `bookaz1sec20` (file `gwh1sec20.pdf`). The second- and third-year PDFs are from item `bookazhar12` (files `twheed2sec.pdf`, `twheed3sec.pdf`). The uploader credits zad4study.blogspot.com. The PDF metadata gives Adobe InDesign CS6, created 15 September 2019.
* Each first-year and second-year introduction says the book is "المقرر على طالب الصف الأول/الثاني الثانوي" (prescribed for students of the first/second secondary year). The first-year introduction calls the commentary "a fruit of the Ash'ari school, the school of Ahl al-Sunna wa'l-Jama'a, which the community received with acceptance" (ثمرة من ثمرات المذهب الأشعري؛ مذهب أهل السنة والجماعة الذي تلقته الأمة بالقبول).

## Second year, pp. 10–11: the relation of causes to effects

The verse of the *Jawhara* on human acts (p. 10):

> وعندنا للعبد كسب كلفا *** ولم يكن مؤثرا فلتعرفا

With us, the servant has an acquisition for which he is made responsible, but he is not an effective cause; so know it.

Scriptural proof given: Qur'an 37:96, "God created you and what you do," and the hadith "God is the maker of every maker and his making."

The four positions (pp. 10–11):

> المذاهب في علاقة الأسباب بالمسببات:
> ١ـ من اعتقد أن الأسباب العادية كالنار، والسكين، والأكل، والشرب تؤثر في مسبباتها كالحرق، والقطع، والشبع، والري بطبعها وذاتها، فهو كافر بالإجماع.
> ٢ـ ومن اعتقد أنها تؤثر بقوة خلقها الله فيها. كما يقول المعتزلة: إن العبد يخلق أفعال نفسه الاختيارية بقدرة أودعها الله فيه، فهو فاسق مبتدع.
> ٣ـ ومن اعتقد أن المؤثر هو الله، لكن جعل بين الأسباب ومسبباتها تلازمًا عقليًّا، بحيث لا يصح تخلفها، فهو جاهل، وربما جرَّه ذلك إلى الكفر، فقد ينكر معجزات الأنبياء لكونها على خلاف العادة.
> ٤ـ ومن اعتقد أن المؤثر هو الله، وجعل بين الأسباب والمسببات تلازمًا عاديًّا، بحيث يصح تخلفها، فهو المؤمن الناجي إن شاء الله.

The positions on the relation of causes to effects:

1. Whoever believes that ordinary causes, such as fire, the knife, eating, and drinking, produce their effects, such as burning, cutting, satiety, and the quenching of thirst, by their nature and essence, is an unbeliever by consensus.
2. Whoever believes that they produce their effects by a power God created in them, as the Mu'tazila say that the servant creates his own voluntary acts by a power God placed in him, is a sinner and an innovator.
3. Whoever believes that the one who produces the effect is God, but makes the connection between causes and their effects a rational necessity, such that the effect cannot fail to follow, is ignorant, and that may lead him to unbelief, for he may deny the miracles of the prophets, since they are contrary to custom.
4. Whoever believes that the one who produces the effect is God, and makes the connection between causes and effects a connection of custom, such that the effect can fail to follow, is the believer who is saved, if God wills.

## Second year, p. 82: conditions of a miracle

> ٢ـ أن تكون على خلاف العادة، وهي ما اعتاده الناس واستمروا عليه مرة بعد أخرى، فالمعجزة لا تخضع لأسباب، ولا تنتج عن مقدمات، فما كان كذلك فليس من المعجزة في شيء كتمدد المعادن بالحرارة وانكماشها بالبرودة.

2. That it be contrary to custom, which is what people have become accustomed to and have continued in, time after time. The miracle is not subject to causes and does not result from premises. Whatever is of that kind is no miracle at all, such as the expansion of metals with heat and their contraction with cold.

Condition 3 on the same page: the miracle must appear at the hand of one who claims prophecy, "so as to be a confirmation of him in his claim".

## First year, p. 80: hearing defined by custom

> السمع يطلق في الحوادث على: القوة المودعة في صماخ الأذن لتدرك بها الأصوات عادة.

In created beings, "hearing" means the faculty placed in the ear canal by which sounds are perceived, by custom.
