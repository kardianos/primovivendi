## United States

### Declaration of Independence
- **File**: `usa/declaration_of_independence_1776.pdf`
- **Citable Name**: "The Declaration of Independence, July 4, 1776"
- **Original Source**: National Archives and Records Administration (archives.gov)
- **Date Acquired**: 
- **Language**: English (original)
- **Notes**: Official transcript from the National Archives. Primary foundational document showing emphasis on rights derived from "Laws of Nature and of Nature's God" and grievances based on lack of representation (procedural symmetry).

### Federalist Papers (selected)
- **File**: `usa/federalist_papers_madison_10_51.pdf`
- **Citable Name**: "The Federalist No. 10" and "The Federalist No. 51" by James Madison
- **Original Source**: Library of Congress / Founders Online
- **Date Acquired**: 
- **Language**: English
- **Notes**: Key documents on factions, human nature, and separation of powers as responses to human limits.

### Letters on the Constitution (Madison & Jefferson)
- **File**: `usa/madison_jefferson_letters_constitution.pdf`
- **Citable Name**: Selected correspondence between James Madison and Thomas Jefferson, 1787–1789
- **Original Source**: Founders Online (National Archives)
- **Date Acquired**: 
- **Language**: English
- **Notes**: Shows the actual concerns and aims behind the Constitution — representation, checks on power, and grounding in reality rather than abstract theory.

## Singapore / Lee Kuan Yew

### The Singapore Story (Memoirs)
- **File**: `singapore/lee_kuan_yew_the_singapore_story_1998.pdf`
- **Citable Name**: Lee Kuan Yew, *The Singapore Story: Memoirs of Lee Kuan Yew* (1998)
- **Original Source**: Published by Times Editions / Marshall Cavendish
- **Date Acquired**: 
- **Language**: English (Lee's primary writing language)
- **Notes**: First volume of Lee's memoirs. Primary source on his thinking during the early years and merger with Malaysia.

### From Third World to First
- **File**: `singapore/lee_kuan_yew_from_third_world_to_first_2000.pdf`
- **Citable Name**: Lee Kuan Yew, *From Third World to First: The Singapore Story 1965–2000* (2000)
- **Original Source**: HarperCollins
- **Date Acquired**: 
- **Language**: English
- **Notes**: Second volume. Details the deliberate construction of institutions, rejection of outcome symmetry, and tiered priority thinking.

**Note on original language**: Lee Kuan Yew primarily wrote and spoke in English. Some major speeches exist in Chinese and Malay; these will be sourced separately if needed for specific quotes.

## Iran (1978–1983 period)

**Target**:
- Writings and letters by Khomeini and other Islamists (e.g., "Islamic Government")
