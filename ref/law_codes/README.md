## Laws of Hammurabi

Two public-domain translations agree on every law used:

* **King**: L. W. King, translation of 1910, as served by the Avalon Project (`https://avalon.law.yale.edu/ancient/hamcode.asp`), saved as `dl/avalon_hamcode.html`.
* **Johns**: C. H. W. Johns, *The Oldest Code of Laws in the World* (T. & T. Clark, 1903), Project Gutenberg 17150, saved as `dl/pg17150.txt`.

King renders the middle rank (*muškēnum*) as "freed man" and Johns as "poor man"; Chapter 9 says "commoner". For laws 198 and 201 King has "gold mina" where Johns has "mina of silver"; Chapter 9 follows Johns (one-third of a mina is twenty shekels).

| Law | King (1910) | Johns (1903) |
|---|---|---|
| 196 | If a man put out the eye of another man, his eye shall be put out. | If a man has caused the loss of a gentleman's eye, his eye one shall cause to be lost. |
| 197 | If he break another man's bone, his bone shall be broken. | If he has shattered a gentleman's limb, one shall shatter his limb. |
| 198 | If he put out the eye of a freed man, or break the bone of a freed man, he shall pay one gold mina. | If he has caused a poor man to lose his eye or shattered a poor man's limb, he shall pay one mina of silver. |
| 199 | If he put out the eye of a man's slave, or break the bone of a man's slave, he shall pay one-half of its value. | If he has caused the loss of the eye of a gentleman's servant or has shattered the limb of a gentleman's servant, he shall pay half his price. |
| 200 | If a man knock out the teeth of his equal, his teeth shall be knocked out. | If a man has made the tooth of a man that is his equal to fall out, one shall make his tooth fall out. |
| 201 | If he knock out the teeth of a freed man, he shall pay one-third of a gold mina. | If he has made the tooth of a poor man to fall out, he shall pay one-third of a mina of silver. |
| 209 | If a man strike a free-born woman so that she lose her unborn child, he shall pay ten shekels for her loss. | If a man has struck a gentleman's daughter and caused her to drop what is in her womb, he shall pay ten shekels of silver for what was in her womb. |
| 210 | If the woman die, his daughter shall be put to death. | If that woman has died, one shall put to death his daughter. |
| 229 | If a builder build a house for some one, and does not construct it properly, and the house which he built fall in and kill its owner, then that builder shall be put to death. | If a builder has built a house for a man and has not made strong his work, and the house he built has fallen, and he has caused the death of the owner of the house, that builder shall be put to death. |
| 230 | If it kill the son of the owner the son of that builder shall be put to death. | If he has caused the son of the owner of the house to die, one shall put to death the son of that builder. |

Chapter 9 quotes King for laws 210 and 230.

## Middle Assyrian Laws, Tablet A, §55

Translation by Martha T. Roth, *Law Collections from Mesopotamia and Asia Minor* (SBL Writings from the Ancient World), which is under copyright. The wording was checked against the full quotation, credited to Roth, in TheTorah.com, "Marrying Your Daughter to Her Rapist" (saved as `dl/thetorah_marrying.html`). Luckenbill's numbering gives the same law as 54.

The law covers a man who forcibly seizes and rapes an unbetrothed maiden living in her father's house. Roth's rendering of the remedy: the father of the maiden shall take the wife of the man and hand her over to be raped, and shall not return her to her husband. If the man has no wife, he pays "triple" the silver as the value of the maiden and marries her, or the father takes the silver and gives his daughter to whomever he chooses.

Chapter 9 paraphrases the law and does not quote Roth.

## King James Version

Project Gutenberg 10, saved as `dl/kjv_pg10.txt`. Public domain.

* **Genesis 4:23–24:** "And Lamech said unto his wives, Adah and Zillah, Hear my voice; ye wives of Lamech, hearken unto my speech: for I have slain a man to my wounding, and a young man to my hurt. If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamech seventy and sevenfold." Cain is avenged sevenfold; Lamech claims seventy-sevenfold.
* **Deuteronomy 24:16:** "The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, neither shall the children be put to death for the fathers: every man shall be put to death for his own sin."
* **2 Kings 14:5–6** (the rule applied by King Amaziah): "...he slew his servants which had slain the king his father. But the children of the murderers he slew not: according unto that which is written in the book of the law of Moses..."
* **Exodus 21:22–25** (not cited in Chapter 9): the talion follows "he shall pay as the judges determine" (21:22).
* **Leviticus 24:19–22** (not cited in Chapter 9): "as he hath done, so shall it be done to him"; "Ye shall have one manner of law, as well for the stranger, as for one of your own country" (24:22). Leviticus 24:16, in the same passage, sets death for blasphemy.

## Qur'an

Arabic from the Tanzil text (`quran-simple`, via api.alquran.cloud), checked against api.quran.com (`text_imlaei`); the consonants match for all four verses. Saved in `ext-ref/quran/verses/`. Tanzil permits verbatim copies with attribution. The English is made from the Arabic for this review; Saheeh International, Pickthall, and Yusuf Ali are in the saved API responses for comparison.

### 2:178

> يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الْقِصَاصُ فِي الْقَتْلَى ۖ الْحُرُّ بِالْحُرِّ وَالْعَبْدُ بِالْعَبْدِ وَالْأُنْثَىٰ بِالْأُنْثَىٰ ۚ فَمَنْ عُفِيَ لَهُ مِنْ أَخِيهِ شَيْءٌ فَاتِّبَاعٌ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ وَأَدَاءٌ إِلَيْهِ بِإِحْسَانٍ ۗ ذَٰلِكَ تَخْفِيفٌ مِنْ رَبِّكُمْ وَرَحْمَةٌ ۗ فَمَنِ اعْتَدَىٰ بَعْدَ ذَٰلِكَ فَلَهُ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ

**Translation:** You who believe, retaliation is prescribed for you in the case of those killed: the free for the free, the slave for the slave, and the female for the female. Whoever is pardoned in part by his brother, then let the claim be pursued with fairness and paid to him with good conduct. That is a lightening from your Lord and a mercy. Whoever transgresses after that will have a painful punishment.

### 2:179

> وَلَكُمْ فِي الْقِصَاصِ حَيَاةٌ يَا أُولِي الْأَلْبَابِ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ

**Translation:** In retaliation there is life for you, you who have understanding, so that you may guard yourselves.

### 17:33

> وَلَا تَقْتُلُوا النَّفْسَ الَّتِي حَرَّمَ اللَّهُ إِلَّا بِالْحَقِّ ۗ وَمَنْ قُتِلَ مَظْلُومًا فَقَدْ جَعَلْنَا لِوَلِيِّهِ سُلْطَانًا فَلَا يُسْرِفْ فِي الْقَتْلِ ۖ إِنَّهُ كَانَ مَنْصُورًا

**Translation:** Do not kill the soul that God has made sacred, except by right. Whoever is killed wrongfully, We have given his heir authority, so let him not go beyond the limit in killing. He has been given help.

### 5:45

> وَكَتَبْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ فِيهَا أَنَّ النَّفْسَ بِالنَّفْسِ وَالْعَيْنَ بِالْعَيْنِ وَالْأَنْفَ بِالْأَنْفِ وَالْأُذُنَ بِالْأُذُنِ وَالسِّنَّ بِالسِّنِّ وَالْجُرُوحَ قِصَاصٌ ۚ فَمَنْ تَصَدَّقَ بِهِ فَهُوَ كَفَّارَةٌ لَهُ ۚ وَمَنْ لَمْ يَحْكُمْ بِمَا أَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الظَّالِمُونَ

**Translation:** We prescribed for them in it [the Torah]: a life for a life, an eye for an eye, a nose for a nose, an ear for an ear, a tooth for a tooth, and for wounds, retaliation. Whoever forgoes it as charity, it is an expiation for him. Whoever does not judge by what God has sent down, those are the wrongdoers.

Chapter 9 uses 2:178 ("the free for the free, the slave for the slave, and the female for the female"; the heir may forgo retaliation for a payment) and 17:33 (the heir's authority, and the limit on killing).
