# References (committable)

This folder holds the references that may be committed: our own notes, logs, translations and excerpts, and public-domain texts. Full copies of third-party works are in `ext-ref/` at the repo root, which `.gitignore` excludes. The two folders use the same subfolder names, so a note here and its source files sit at matching paths.

Rule for new material: own work and public-domain texts go here. Any copy of a third-party work that is under copyright, or whose status is uncertain, goes in the matching path under `ext-ref/`.

## Contents

* `book1-4/`: the book1-4 primary-source collection, moved from `book1-4/ref/` on 2026-11-10. It holds `README.md`, `SOURCES.md`, `SOURCE_LOG.txt`, the research-gap notes (`gap_*.txt`), and the public-domain USA texts (Declaration of Independence; Federalist 10 and 51). Its third-party copies are in `ext-ref/book1-4/`.
* `ghazali_tahafut_al_falasifa/ch03_excerpts.md`: passages from al-Ghazali's *Tahafut al-Falasifa* and Ibn Rushd's reply, for `civ/chapters/03_ch03.md`, with locations and translations.
* `azhar_curriculum/README.md`: al-Azhar's secondary creed textbook (2019–2020), including the four-way ruling on causes, translated.
* `salafi_causes/README.md`: Ibn Taymiyya, Ibn al-Qayyim, and IslamQA answer 238544 on causes, translated.
* `islam_sunni_salafi/00_summary.md`: the comparison of mainstream Sunni and Salafi positions against the five principles, and on apostates, heretics, and non-Muslims.

Arabic quotations are set in block quotes so that right-to-left text lays out correctly.
