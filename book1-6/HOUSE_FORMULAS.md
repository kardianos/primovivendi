## A1 — Axiom floor (Q1 in one breath)

**Locked:** One public answer to Q1. Reality and truth are **two parts of one whole about reality**, not two coordinate first axioms of equal rank.

**House sense:**

- Truth is the name we give a model of that reality (as against a model of falsehood or of hope): correspondence of claim to what is.

## A2 — Judgment, room, and world (world-as-judge)

**Locked:** Neither bare “keep the world” nor a lab-only caveat alone. The house relation is: **man judges under humility; the world does not sit in the room; the world answers claims about what is and what follows; acceptance is not correspondence.**

**House formula (canonical):**

> Each man must judge, for no one else can do his seeing for him; and each man must judge under humility, for he is limited and often wrong. The world does not take its seat in the room where speeches are scored. When a claim is offered as knowledge of what is, or of what will follow from what is, the world answers by what in fact comes to pass. What the company accepts may still prevail among speakers for a time; it does not alter what comes to pass. Truth is the name of a model of reality, rather than of falsehood or of hope.

**Writer rules:**

- Callbacks to “world wins / keep the world / things win” must keep this **sense** (judgment + humility; room ≠ world; answer in what comes to pass; truth ≠ acceptance). Exact wording may vary; sense may not.
- Does not wipe mystery, divine, or non-testable goods: the world’s answer is for claims offered as knowledge of what is or what follows — not a machine that settles every dispute.
- The good (life, thrival, duty) is affirmed elsewhere; consequence does not invent the good, but neither does the room cancel consequence.
- M1 still sections off what the method does not settle.

**Downstream:** C1 scope language across chapters; align bare maxims to this sense.

## A3 — Positive-first (vision, not contrast)

**Locked:** Positive-first. The work of this book is a **positive vision**, not a contrasting survey of schools.

**House sense:**

- State our answers first and primarily.
- Bounds in Part 1 may contain the vision (“it is this, not that”) as a **guiding light** — a short refuse that orients the reader — not as exposition of alternatives.
- Full packages, steelmans, and rival tours belong later (H8 / Part 2 dossiers), not beside every bold axiom head.
- Required refuses (e.g. blank slate; justice not equal end-states / not return in kind) stay as plain boundary sentences after the positive claim. They do not open the paragraph and do not expand into alternative systems.

**Writer rules:**

- Opener: our answers; other answers exist and appear later — do not pledge “beside chief antagonists” or “not the sole conclusion” as the chapter’s stance.
- Body: positive claim → brief “not that” only where the question demands a boundary → no rival parade.
- “Not that” names the boundary; it does not teach the rejected view.

**Downstream:** B1.3 opener; B1.1–B1.2 refuse-after-positive; charge mirrors the same pattern.

2. Inside a defined membership, role, or jurisdiction (when the bounded set itself must be named).
3. *Scope* alone only when defined in place and doing work.

**Writer / agent rules:**

## A6 — Human premise in 1100 (assert; M4 order; full sense in dictionary)

**Locked:** Reserve empirical fashion. The book **offers a standard for examination**; it does not perform the examination of every fact of the age. **Do not lean on twin studies or other contemporary study language** in the axioms block.

**Canonical full sense:** `plan/dictionary_p1.md` §B7–B8 (especially B7.2 self-interest and B8 particular). Manuscript may phrase smaller; agents must not lose that full sense.

**1100 anthropology (M4):** three positive limbs in order **limited → particular → self-interested** (self-interested last as bridge into near care and later priority). No “token”; no ordered-priority lecture inside this block (priority may look back later); no studies; no dead-earth.

1. **Limited** — finite in time, energy, knowledge, reach; finite in what he can be made into (social force does not turn him into another kind of creature while he means to live).
2. **Particular** — this person, of a family and a chain that formed him, in a situation that is his. (Lineage/ability as full specs → E0+; not 1100 co-heads.)
3. **Self-interested** — cares for own life and well-being, and for life continuing in those who are his and in what he hands on (not first mankind as a faceless crowd); that care is **good**; alone **incomplete** for life among many under shared rules; not a creed of pure egoism.

### Publish choice: Variation A4 (use this)

**Status:** Author-chosen publish target for the 1100 human-premise paragraph (B1). Prefer this wording unless the author revises it.

> **Man is limited, particular, and self-interested.**  
> He is limited, finite in time, energy, knowledge, and reach, and finite in what he can be made into. No social force turns him into another kind of creature while he remains a man who means to live. He is particular, this person, of a family and a chain that formed him, in a situation that is his. He is self-interested, caring for his own life and well-being, and for life continuing in those who are his and in what he hands on, not first for mankind as a crowd without faces or households. That care is good. Alone it is not yet life among many under shared rules; it remains the real motive of a living man, and it only ever runs through someone in particular.

### Alternate: Variation F4 (context / fallback)

**Status:** Author-approved alternate. Keep for context and use only if A4 needs a denser neighbor wording or a fallback during revision. Do not prefer F4 over A4 by default.

> **Man is limited, particular, and self-interested.**  
> He is limited in time, energy, knowledge, and reach, and limited in what he can become. Social force does not make him a different creature; so long as he means to survive, his drive to live does not yield to total remaking as if his nature were empty matter. He is particular: this person, of a family and a chain that formed him, in a situation that is his. He is self-interested: he cares for his own life and well-being, and for that life continuing in those who are his and in what he hands on, not first for the species as a faceless whole. That care is good. Alone it is incomplete for life among many under shared rules. It is still what moves him, and it is always the care of someone in particular.

**Writer rules:**

- Soften or cut “studies of twins reared apart… resembling stations”; close verify by removing unsourced study claims.
- Positive-first (A3); short guiding-light refuse (blank slate / factory goodness) where Q2 requires; no rival parade; no dog-whistle slogan pair on ability+lineage in 1100.
- Implement **A4** in `1100` unless author directs otherwise; align charge list to limited, particular, and self-interested (that order).

## A8 — Style intensity (meaning first; Campbell as clarity, not costume)

**Locked:** Option (i) — **staged.** Content phases (B–C) write clear formal prose, claim-true, without ornament hunt. Phase D applies full W12 / `style/campbell_agent_rules.md` chapter by chapter. Flow (E) last.

**What “Campbell” means for this book (author):**

- The **chief** demand is not period length or 18th-century costume. It is that **every word carry meaning**. If a word can be thrown away without loss, it should not be there (W4 / E3; Campbell on vivacity: fewer words with purity and perspicuity).
- **Metaphors / pictures:** when used, they must be real work — order, logic, vitality (W2) — poetry that earns its place, not lines that could vanish unmissed. Decorative or stacked figures fail A8 and W2.
- Careful emulation of Campbell’s **habits** (define, divide, discriminate; one principal end; perspicuity first) **increases clarity**, which serves the work. That is the point.
- **Not the goal:** “sound like Campbell” as pastiche. **The goal:** each word chosen carefully; each concept dictated clearly.

**Writer rules by phase:**

| Phase | Intensity |
|-------|-----------|
| **B–C (content)** | Clear, formal, complete sentences. House formulas’ sense intact. No forced archaism. Every word still load-bearing — plain is not license for slop. |
| **D (style)** | Full W12 read of Campbell rules + examples; balanced periods where they aid clarity; protect refuses and FIX_PLAN §2 blocks; never delete meaning for euphony. |
| **E (flow)** | Joints and arcs only; no new doctrine; no emptying words for smooth transitions. |

## A9 — Adversary naming, attribution, diction, and base rates (author 2026-07-24)

**Status:** Author-locked. Supersedes plan P2.F1 / H8 wording where it taught soft “persons are not packages” as refusal to attribute professed creeds.

### A9.1 What this book’s answers are called

Call this book’s load-bearing answers **axioms**, **principles**, **answers**, or **core principles** — not a “package.”

**“Package”** (if used at all) names an **adversary** set of premises, texts, and institutions under examination — not the house catechism. Prefer plain names of the adversary system when clearer (constructivism, outcome equity, Islamic political doctrine, etc.). Do not use *package* as a synonym for “principles” or “what someone believes in general.”

### A9.2 Attribution of professed creed (strengthened 2026-07-24)

- If a person **freely proclaims** allegiance to a theology, sect, ideology, or program, it is **socially good and acceptable** to hold them to **what that system announces** as its values and political content — including hard content (violence norms, anti-life eco programs, totalizing law, etc.).
- **Do not** require proof that *this* person personally practices every hard clause before the profession counts. Profession is allegiance to the announced ideas.
- **Do not** empty labels with long hedges (“labels are not emptied…”, “court and moral… blood alone…”, “not by surname…”) in every dossier. Those guards are weak sauce. Cut them. Stay on the **consequence of ideas**.
- Drop the slogan **“persons are not packages.”** Do not reintroduce multi-sentence blood/surname apologetics.
- **Court of law** (same procedure, evidence of acts) remains distinct from **social attribution of proclaimed allegiance**. Do not confuse the two; do not write three paragraphs every time to prove you are not blood-grading.

### A9.3 Blood, surnames, and statistical caution

- Do not collapse into **ethnic grading as moral fate**. Blood is not the topic when the topic is **professed creed**.
- **Group base rates** for **safekeeping** under limits remain legitimate (age, sex, place, measured crime patterns). Ignoring rates is not virtue.
- “Non-political” personal culture can still be politics when it aligns with an enemy program.

### A9.4 “Rival” diction — ban as house cant

**Do not** use **rival** for conflicting epistemologies, doctrines, or justice theories. Peers race as rivals; **truth is not a race**.

For each former “rival,” name what is meant:
- different / opposing / conflicting **principles** or **answers**
- a **specific system** (constructivism, outcome equity, Islamic political doctrine, Qur’an/classical political reading, progressive program, etc.)
- **disfavored model** / **other account** (inquiry method)
- Keep **quoted** Madison “rival interests” and ordinary peer sense (“a rival for the rank”) when that is the real meaning.

### A9.5 Chapter form for adversary sections

- Arc map; short dossiers; **steelman then conflict** without defensive prefaces.
- Temporary allies ≠ shared axioms — one line, not a sermon.
- Prefer concrete system names over “package” and never over “rival.”

**Downstream:** A9b rewrites: strip rival; cut blood-guard; shorten profession attribution to plain force; 1800, 2600, 2000, 2200, 2500, 2700, 1500, 1200, 2300 as needed.
