New issues only. Settled decisions from `notes/07_ccp_sheet_calls.md` were applied in both files and are not repeated here.

**3. The point is population replacement.** A large cohort taken from one society and culture tends to reproduce that society and culture in a different land. If the people are able, they adopt the culture of the place they enter. If they are unable, they recreate the failures of the culture they carried. Applied in both files. The "next morning" and "instantly" versions are gone.

**4. Applied.** "The only institution in history" is dropped. The household is the one that feeds and forms its own children at personal cost.

**5. Applied as suggested.** The universal "every regime" and "terror" are gone. A design that requires human beings to have no sex, no particular attachments, and no private motives fails in operation, and holding it in place has required coercion.

**6. Applied, and revelation stays under test.** The derivation now allows revelation to add a claim about what the senses do not reach, and forbids it to cancel a measurement. The obligation to test the revelation remains. The same sentence is in `ccp_eng.md` and `ccp_sheet.md`.

## 1. Lysenko and "millions starved"

**Where:** `ccp_derive.md`, Tangent 1, item 3.

**Wording:** "In the 1930s and 1940s, the Soviet Union placed Trofim Lysenko in charge of agricultural biology because his theories of acquired traits matched Marxist ideology, while imprisoning geneticists who aligned with truth. The physical crops did not obey Marxist theory; they died, and millions of human beings starved to death."

**Problem:** Lysenko's rise, the suppression of geneticists, and serious crop failure are real. The sentence makes him the cause of "millions" of starvation deaths across the 1930s and 1940s. The great famines are not one program of one biologist. The 1932–1933 famine is earlier and wider than Lysenko's control of agronomy. Later shortages have more than one cause. The Chernobyl sentence in the same item is a usable case: a turbine test was run with safety systems disabled, and the core did not follow the administrative schedule.

**What to do:** Keep Chernobyl. Narrow Lysenko to what the example needs: a state put an ideologically convenient biology in charge, punished the geneticists, and the crops did not obey the theory. Do not assign the famines' dead to that appointment in one clause.

## 2. The sex-difference examples add injury rates and fertility

**Where:** `ccp_derive.md`, Tangent 2. `ccp_eng.md`, Chapter 2.1, "Interchangeability of the Sexes."

**Wording:** Derive says that if men and women were interchangeable, "men and women should be interchangeable in combat infantry units carrying eighty-pound packs, and biological mothers and fathers should be interchangeable in nursing infants. When tested, female soldiers suffer stress fractures at multiples of the male rate under heavy load, and fertility drops when family roles are treated as purely administrative assignments." Eng says standards in combat infantry or heavy labor "either lower operational capability or cause disproportionate physical injury, while simultaneously disrupting family formation."

**Problem:** The sheet already refuses to treat men and women as interchangeable administrative units. These sentences add a pack-weight test, a fracture multiple, and a fertility drop. "Multiples of the male rate" has no study under it. "Fertility drops when family roles are treated as purely administrative assignments" does not say whose fertility, or how the administrative treatment causes it. Nursing infants is a real reproductive difference and does not need the injury statistic.

**What to do:** Keep the refusal of interchangeability. If a physical example stays, state the difference the sheet already owns (reproductive function, and average strength where the task is carrying a load) and drop the unstated multiple and the fertility mechanism, unless a source is going to be named.

## 3. Population replacement as an immediate institutional failure

**Where:** `ccp_derive.md`, Tangent 2. `ccp_eng.md`, Chapter 2.4, "Interchangeable-Token Egalitarianism."

**Wording:** Derive says you should be able to "take ten thousand people from a society with no tradition of written contract law or industrial maintenance, place them into a high-trust municipal administration or an aviation maintenance facility, and expect identical institutional output the next morning." Eng says policies assume "any population can be replaced or mixed with any other population instantly without altering the functioning of institutions, laws, or social trust," and that treating humans as interchangeable tokens "disrupts social cooperation and degrades institutional performance."

**Problem:** Particularity in the sheet is a fact about a person: sex, genetic lineage, language, family, and the culture he was raised in. These passages scale that fact to a rule about moving or mixing populations, and they make the failure immediate ("the next morning," "instantly"). Language and inherited method do change what a crew can do on day one. The passages do not say how large the movement is, whether the people are taught the method, or how long the teaching takes. "Any population" and "instantly" do more work than the sheet's particularity.

**What to do:** Decide whether particularity here is only the person (the sheet), or also a claim about replacing a population inside an institution. If the second claim stays, name the condition that does the damage: the method is not yet held by the people who must run the machines. Drop "the next morning" and "any population" unless that strong version is intended.

## 4. The family as the only institution that has ever raised children

**Where:** `ccp_derive.md`, Tangent 3, equal-allocation utilitarianism.

**Wording:** "the family unit (the only institution in history that reliably raises children with personal care)."

**Problem:** The tier argument does not need a historical universal. Households are the near place where children are fed and formed. Other arrangements have raised particular children, including adoption and kin, which Virtue 7 already keeps open. "The only institution in history" is a broader claim than the tiers.

**What to do:** Say that the household is the institution that reliably feeds and forms its own children at personal cost. Cut "the only institution in history."

## 5. Every regime that re-engineers human nature uses terror

**Where:** `ccp_eng.md`, Chapter 2.1, "Theories of Complete Malleability."

**Wording:** "Every political regime that has attempted to re-engineer human nature on this premise has had to rely on continuous coercion and terror, because the biological traits of the population continually resist the artificial design."

**Problem:** The sheet already rules out treating humans as blank slates that a state can reshape. "Every" regime, and "terror" as the necessary means, is a separate historical universal. Coercion shows up in famous cases. The sentence does not need the universal to make the engineering point, which is that a design for a different kind of human fails when actual humans operate it.

**What to do:** Say that a design which requires humans to have no sex, no particular attachments, and no private motives fails in operation, and that holding that design in place has required coercion. Drop "every" and "terror" unless the universal is going to be argued.

## 6. The derivation never allows revelation

**Where:** `ccp_derive.md`, the knowability step in Section 2. `ccp_eng.md` now states the allowance. `ccp_sheet.md` already states it.

**Wording:** Derive concludes that reality is knowable "through sensory observation and reason together," and then moves on. It does not say what the sheet says: revelation may add a claim about what the senses do not reach, and it may not cancel a measurement.

**Problem:** A reader of the derivation can think knowability is only measurement. The short sheet allows one further source, with that limit. The engineering file now carries the allowance. The derivation does not.

**What to do:** Add two sentences at the knowability step, the same limit as the sheet. Do not reopen the neglect cases. Sensory contact and reason stay linked.
