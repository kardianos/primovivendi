## Final Rewrite (Single Paragraph, 8 Sentences, 131 Words)

> This book takes each faith and philosophy at its word. It rejects the claim that all religions point to one nebulous god and that their differences are small. That claim takes no creed at its word and in the end affirms nothing. Creeds contradict one another on God, the soul, and the highest good, and not all of them can be true. Yet this book is not a theology, nor is it written to diminish any faith. Believer and atheist alike can use this work without denying or speaking against their convictions. The book asks only what follows from living in one reality with people who believe otherwise. It offers a standard and a guide for civilization: a way for any people to judge what keeps and builds its common life.
