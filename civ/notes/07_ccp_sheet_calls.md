# Calls on `ccp_sheet.md`

The sections below are the original calls and the wording that was proposed. The author's decisions are recorded here. All twenty-five decisions are applied in `civ/ccp_sheet.md`.

The proposed replacements were written in the register of `notes/06_editing_guide.md`: declarative, no em-dash, no exclamation mark.

`notes/06_editing_guide.md` locks these lines, and `notes/00_civ_authoritative_bones.md` records them:

- The five principle sentences, including "Justice is scoped procedural symmetry."
- The seven virtue names, including "Take ownership, which is responsibility" and "Form and listen."

Virtue 7 was "Generational obligation." The author authorized a shorter name that states both acts. The sheet, this note's lock line, `notes/06_editing_guide.md`, `notes/00_civ_authoritative_bones.md`, `ccp_derive.md`, and `ccp_eng.md` now say "Form and listen." `ccp_book.md` and the chapters still say "Generational obligation."

## 1. Honesty is ranked as a surplus

**Where:** Point 4, "Tiered," why it is important.

**Wording:** "fifth, practice basic honesty in his dealings and offer charity toward distant strangers when a surplus allows."

**Problem:** The tiers rank time, money, and energy. Honesty is not spare money. "Align with truth" and Point 5 require it at every tier. As written, straight dealing ranks below paying taxes, and the closing "when a surplus allows" can be read as covering the honesty as well as the charity.

**Put this in place of the whole why-paragraph:**

Because human time, money, and energy are limited, claims on those resources must be ranked by proximity and bond. Nearer tiers rightly command the larger share. Farther tiers command less. In a household budget, a man must first keep himself healthy and employed. Second, he must feed and house his wife and children, and educate his children. Third, he must meet his obligations to extended family, neighbors, and his local community. Fourth, he must perform the duties he owes his country, including taxes levied by law for the country's continuance. Fifth, he may give charity to distant strangers when a surplus remains. Honesty in speech and dealing is not one of these tiers. It binds inside every tier. Shortage of money does not license a lie, and a stranger does not thereby gain the child's claim on the household.

**Also add to "What it cannot accept," after the sentence on grocery money:**

It cannot accept treating truthfulness as an outer-tier surplus, owed only after the household and the state have been satisfied.

## 3. Ownership is both a prior step and the same thing as responsibility

**Where:** Point 2, "Self-interested," why it is important. The virtue name "Take ownership, which is responsibility" stays.

**Wording:** "Valuing your own life is a prerequisite for taking ownership of your own life, and taking ownership is a prerequisite for taking responsibility."

**Problem:** This makes three steps: value your life, then own it, then become responsible for it. Virtue 2 says ownership is responsibility. A sequence and an identity cannot both be the relation between the same two words.

**Put this in place of those two sentences:**

Valuing your own life is what makes ownership possible. Ownership is responsibility: the man answers for his own life rather than leaving it for others to carry. Being self-interested is not a moral defect. It is the ordinary condition from which he takes that responsibility and ceases to be a burden on the people who are doing their own work.

## 4. A code "fails" if its people die

**Where:** Point 3, "Goodness must include," why it is important.

**Wording:** "If a moral or political code results in the death of the people who practice it, or the ruin of their ability to sustain themselves, that code fails the test of reality."

**Problem:** "Results in" covers two different events. A code that commands extinction fails. A people who keep a sound code and are killed by an enemy do not thereby refute the code. The enemy section already depends on that distinction.

**Put this in its place:**

If a moral or political code itself brings about the death of the people who practice it, or ruins their ability to sustain themselves and to raise the next generation, that code fails the test of reality. Death at an enemy's hands is a different fact, and it belongs under Point 4. A people can keep a sound code and still be destroyed by someone who has set out to destroy them. The code fails when its own commands produce the death or the ruin.

## 5. "Technology" means a stable life, and also a method

**Where:** The opening, and "Civilization as the root technology," why it is important.

**Wording:** "A stable life, a stable farm, a stable house, and tools are technology." Later: "A technology is any repeatable method and structure built by humans to solve problems in reality."

**Problem:** The opening calls the stable life and the stable house technology. The later sentence calls technology the method and the structure. A stable life is a condition kept by a method. The two sentences do not say the same thing. Both sentences are in `notes/00_civ_authoritative_bones.md`. Leave the opening intact unless that file is amended with it.

**Put this in place of the later definition sentence:**

A technology is a repeatable method, together with the structure that method keeps in being, by which humans answer a problem in reality. A stable house is technology because a known method built it and keeps it standing. A stable life is technology in the same sense: it continues because conduct is repeated, not because it was wished for.

The rest of that paragraph, from "We commonly think of technology as machines," can stand.

## 6. The tier order is fixed, then declared open

**Where:** Point 4, "Tiered," the why-paragraph and the range paragraph.

**Problem:** The why-paragraph gives a five-step order. The range paragraph says reasonable people still argue about the order of the intermediate tiers. Once call 1 is applied, the why-paragraph still gives one order. The range paragraph should say what is settled and what is open.

**Put this in place of the range paragraph:**

Cultures and households argue over the order of the intermediate tiers: whether a close friend outranks a distant cousin, and how an obligation to a church ranks beside an obligation to the town. The order above is one workable household budget. It does not close that argument. What stands is the shape of the ranking. The self and the household come before the stranger, and a claim on resources thins as it moves outward.

## 7. "The same legal position" is too flat

**Where:** Point 5, "Scoped," the secular-state sentence.

**Wording:** "placing all citizens in the same legal position before the state."

**Problem:** The paragraph's own point is that scope contains different positions: teacher and student, citizen and non-citizen, and later age and competence. A secular state refuses to make religion the ground of a different position. It does not place every citizen in one position for every legal purpose.

**Put this in place of the "By contrast" sentence:**

By contrast, a secular constitutional state defines citizenship without regard to religion, so that religion does not by itself put citizens into different legal positions. Age, competence, office, and the difference between citizen and non-citizen still create different positions. Symmetry runs between persons who occupy the same position.

**Problem:** The home invader, two paragraphs earlier, is one man using force now. He has no program and no target people. The definition that follows is narrower than the section's first example.

## 9. Revelation is allowed, and intuition is refused, with no line between them

**Where:** Point 1, "Knowable," range of views and what it cannot accept.

**Wording:** Revelation is included "provided it does not contradict observable facts." Intuition or emotional certainty cannot "substitute for sensory and empirical verification."

**Problem:** A revelation that says more than observation says is neither a sensory verification nor a contradiction of one. It is inside the range and outside the fence. The two clauses do not draw one line.

**Put this in place of the revelation sentence in the range paragraph:**

Some also count religious revelation as knowledge of what the senses do not reach, provided it does not contradict observable facts in the shared world. Revelation may add a claim. It may not cancel a measurement.

**Put this in place of the intuition sentence:**

It cannot accept intuition or emotional certainty as a substitute for sensory and empirical verification where the claim is about the shared physical world. A feeling that a beam will hold is not a test of the beam. Intuition may suggest a hypothesis. It does not confirm one.

## 10. The Young Turks, 1915–1923, and the 1940s are one act of one actor

**Where:** "Enemies are real," the national-level paragraph.

**Problem:** The events are real. The paragraph binds them into one program of one subject, "the Young Turks and the subsequent Turkish nationalist regimes," aimed at Greeks and Armenians, "to eliminate them from the country," across both 1915–1923 and the 1940s.

Split them:

- The Young Turks were not the government of 1941 or 1942. The later measures belong to the Republic under İnönü.
- 1915 and the years through 1923 include massacre and deportation, and also the 1923 population exchange, which was a treaty and was mutual. Those are not one policy.
- The 1941–1942 labor conscription (the Twenty Classes) and the November 1942 *Varlık Vergisi*, with non-payers sent to labor camps, were expropriation, discriminatory conscription, and ruin of households. They also fell on Jews and Assyrians. They were not the deportations and massacres of 1915.
- "Consequently" has to carry all of that as one demonstration. The principle does not need the events to be one event.

**Put this in place of the national-level paragraph.** Then use the definition sentence from call 8.

At the national level, a state can make itself the enemy of people living under its law. In 1915 the Young Turk government deported and massacred Armenians inside the Ottoman Empire. Greek communities were attacked and expelled across that decade, including by the nationalist governments that followed the Young Turks, through the destruction in Asia Minor. Removal by massacre and deportation is one act. The mutual population exchange of 1923 was a treaty, and it is not the same act. A generation later the Turkish Republic did something narrower and still hostile. In 1941 and 1942 it conscripted non-Muslim men, Armenians, Greeks, Jews, and Assyrians, into unarmed labor service, the Twenty Classes. In November 1942 it imposed the *Varlık Vergisi*, a capital tax assessed far more heavily on non-Muslim citizens, and it sent many who could not pay to labor camps. Those measures ruined households and pushed a people out of the country's economic life. They were not a second 1915. They show the same principle by a different act. A government becomes the enemy of a people under its law when it uses taxation, conscription, confiscation, or violence to harm, dispossess, or destroy them. The enemy is identified by the act, not by treating several governments and several decades as one agent.

## 11. "Many Muslim-majority countries" now operate the classical bundle

**Where:** Point 5, "Scoped," the Islamic-law illustration.

**Wording:** "in many Muslim-majority countries operating under Islamic law, Muslims and non-Muslims are assigned different legal rights regarding taxation, court testimony, family law, and holding high office." And: "Islam operates as a polity that incorporates a religion."

**Problem:** Classical Islamic law did draw those four distinctions, including the jizya and restrictions on non-Muslim testimony and high office. Present tense, aimed at "many Muslim-majority countries," overstates the current map. Religion-based family law, and a requirement that the head of state be Muslim, are widespread. The jizya and unequal criminal testimony are not the ordinary current practice. "A polity that incorporates a religion" also reverses the usual description and states the reversal as a fact.

**Put this in place of the illustration, from "For example, in many Muslim-majority countries" through "who occupies which position within it."**

For example, classical Islamic law defined membership in the political community by religious adherence. In that law, Muslims and non-Muslims held different positions in taxation, through the jizya, in the weight of court testimony, in family law, and in eligibility for the highest offices. Some Muslim-majority states still keep parts of that pattern, in particular religion-based family law and a requirement that the head of state be a Muslim. Many do not levy the jizya, and many do not apply a different rule of criminal testimony. The illustration is the classical arrangement, not a census of present states. In that arrangement Islam is a religion that also constitutes a polity, and full membership in the political scope is defined by religious adherence. By contrast, a secular constitutional state defines citizenship without regard to religion, so that religion does not by itself put citizens into different legal positions. Age, competence, office, and citizenship still do, as the following paragraphs say. Both cases show one structural fact. Before symmetry can be applied, a polity defines the boundary of its scope and who occupies which position inside it.

The secular-state sentence here already includes the repair from call 7. Apply calls 7 and 11 together, once.

## 18. "Scoped procedural symmetry" does not read as three coequal requirements

**Where:** Point 5, the opening why-paragraph. The principle line stays.

**Problem:** In the phrase, "symmetry" is the noun. "Scoped" and "procedural" modify it. The gloss says the three words are one concept and that removing any one of them destroys justice. A reader can think only symmetry is the substance, and that the other two words are optional adjectives. The grammar will not be fixed by rewriting the locked line. The gloss has to state the job of each word.

**Put this immediately after the principle is restated in that section, before "If any one of the three words is removed":**

The phrase is one name for three requirements. "Symmetry" is the noun. "Scoped" and "procedural" name the kind of symmetry that counts as justice. They are not optional description.

Then the existing "without scope / without procedure / without symmetry" sentences can stand. They already do the rest of the work.

## 20. Neglect cases are offered as proof about the senses

**Where:** Point 1, "Knowable."

**Wording:** "Man cannot know the world apart from his senses; terrible cases of severe child neglect demonstrate that a human being deprived of sensory input and interaction cannot develop normally or intrinsically know the world."

**Problem:** The semicolon presents the cases as the demonstration. Children in those cases were deprived of care, language, and interaction. They were not deprived of eyes and ears alone. The cases concern development. They do not prove the claim about the senses. The airplane sentence that follows is the actual demonstration, and it should carry that weight.

**Put this in place of the two sentences that begin "Man cannot know" and end with "reliable processes."**

Man comes to know the world through his senses, together with the language and teaching that train him to use them. Severe neglect shows that a child deprived of care and interaction does not develop a normal knowledge of the world. It does not show that sensation alone is the whole of knowing, because those children were not deprived only of eyes and ears. What shows that sense and reason reach reality is ordinary successful practice. People fly in airplanes and use telephones because engineers have learned the properties of materials and have built processes that work.

## 21. "Capable, virtuous" non-reproduction is replacement or conquest

**Where:** Virtue 6, "Have children."

**Wording:** "If the capable, virtuous members of a civilization do not have children, the population shrinks, ages, and is eventually replaced or conquered by others."

**Problem:** The virtue is that living people must have children if the civilization is to continue. This sentence adds two further claims: that the duty is specially about the capable and the virtuous, and that the arrival of another people is conquest. Neither claim is required by the virtue, and neither is argued.

**Put this in place of that sentence:**

If its members do not have children, the population shrinks and ages, and in time others occupy what those members no longer keep.

**If the selective claim is intended, do not leave it inside this sentence.** Give it a separate paragraph that states the evidence and the limit: who counts as capable, what "replaced" means, and why a neighbor's children are or are not a conquest. Until that paragraph exists, the sentence above is the one that belongs in the virtue.

## 22. "Pacifism" is rejected as such

**Where:** "Enemies are real," what it cannot accept.

**Wording:** "It cannot accept pacifism or the naive belief that enmity is merely a misunderstanding that vanishes if one refuses to name it."

**Problem:** The reason given rejects the claim that enmity disappears when unnamed. The word "pacifism" rejects every position that goes by that name, including a person who will not kill and who still admits that the enemy exists.

**Put this in its place:**

It cannot accept the claim that enmity is only a misunderstanding, or that a threat ceases because one refuses to name it. A person may refuse to kill and still tell the truth that an enemy exists. What this principle rejects is the refusal to recognize an enemy who is acting to destroy the household or the nation, and the refusal to resist him.

The second sentence of the fence, about not labeling every foreigner, poor person, or lawful dissenter an enemy, can stand.

## 23. Beauty requires vitality, and the fence names "deformity"

**Where:** Virtue 4, "Admire beauty," the definition and the fence.

**Wording:** "Beauty is the intersection of vitality, order, and truth." The first example is "a healthy body." The fence rejects art that celebrates "chaos, filth, deformity, or falsehood."

**Problem:** If vitality means a healthy, intact subject, a tragedy, an elegy, or a true picture of a wound falls outside beauty. The file gives no sign that this exclusion is intended. "Deformity," set beside filth and falsehood as something one must not celebrate, also leaves depiction and celebration undistinguished.

**Put this in place of the definition sentence and the two example sentences that follow it, through "cannot be truly beautiful."** Keep the sentences that begin "Admiring beauty is practical."

Beauty is vitality, order, and truth showing together in a particular thing. Vitality means that the thing has living form: a healthy body, a building with a coherent structure, an engine running as designed, a sentence that moves. It does not mean that the subject must be cheerful or intact. A tragedy is beautiful when its form is alive and its account of grief is true. A fictional tale that truly describes a moral reality, or an aspect of human nature, can be beautiful. A tale that depicts reality falsely, such as one that portrays cowardice and decay as noble and free of consequence, cannot.

This wording includes the grammatical repair from call 15.

**Put this in place of the fence sentence that begins "It cannot accept the cult of ugliness":**

It cannot accept the cult of ugliness: art, architecture, or narratives that celebrate chaos, filth, or falsehood. Depicting a wound, a ruin, or a deformed body truthfully is a different act from celebrating it. It also cannot accept treating beauty as mere idle luxury detached from function and truth.

## 25. What belongs to everyone "quickly" falls into disrepair

**Where:** Virtue 2, "Take ownership, which is responsibility."

**Wording:** "Property and tasks that belong to "everyone in general" belong to no one in particular, and quickly fall into disrepair."

**Problem:** The point is that a thing with no named keeper goes unkept. "Quickly" and the unrestricted plural say that every unnamed common holding fails, and fails fast. Some shared works last, and they last because a particular person or a particular office answers for them.

**Put this in its place:**

Property and tasks assigned to everyone in general are assigned to no one in particular. Unless a named person, or a named office, answers for them, they fall into disrepair. A shared work lasts only when someone in particular is charged with the upkeep.
