## 1. Epistemology, Three Anti-Correspondence Doctrines, and Discovered Technology (Principle 1)
- **Surfacing Hidden Premises:**
  - Surface policy debates (e.g., two neighbors arguing over prosecuting repeat shoplifters vs. therapy, or school board parents arguing over clear discipline vs. restorative circles) cannot be resolved by trading statistics when the speakers hold opposite underlying premises about reality, human nature, goodness, priority, and justice.
- **Three Named Doctrines That Sever Truth from Reality (and Thus Disable Civilization's Method):**
  1. **Scripturalism (e.g., W. Gary Crampton / Gordon Clark tradition):** Holds that a proposition is true solely because God thinks it or states it in scripture, denying that human empirical observation of the physical world is a valid source of knowledge or verification. While sounding pious, this severs truth from correspondence to observable reality: no physical practice can be tested and no empirical error corrected if sensory observation is rejected as an epistemic ground.
  2. **Ash'arite Occasionalism (Al-Ghazali, dominant in classical Sunni theology):** Holds that God is the direct, immediate cause of every physical event at every instant—fire does not burn cotton through any stable physical property or secondary natural law; God simply wills the burning anew each time and could will otherwise at any moment. This denies intrinsic causal regularity in nature, removing the foundation for scientific testing and predictable physical consequence.
  3. **Postmodernism / Deconstructionism (Michel Foucault, Jacques Derrida):** Holds that reality, truth, and knowledge are linguistic conventions and artifacts of social power. Language ceases to be an instrument for describing an independent world and becomes purely a weapon for moving power.
  - *Structural Unity of the Three:* Whether truth is subordinated to unobservable textual decree (Scripturalism), unbound instantaneous will denying natural cause-and-effect (Occasionalism), or social power (Postmodernism), all three remove the external physical check against which models must be tested.
- **Incomplete Models vs. Wrong Models (Aristotle → Newton → Einstein vs. Alchemy):**
  - *Incomplete:* Aristotle's mechanics described everyday terrestrial motion within narrow bounds; Newton generalized it to planetary and engineering scales; Einstein generalized it to relativistic speeds. None made the earlier model "a lie" within its operational tolerances.
  - *Wrong:* Alchemy promised for centuries to transmute lead into gold by chemical recipes and failed every test in every laboratory.
- **Systems as Compensators for Human Limits:**
  - How do limited, fallible, self-interested humans achieve reliable truth and order? By building **procedural systems** where individual limits check each other:
    - *A daily written diary* compensates for self-flattering, fading human memory.
    - *Double-entry bookkeeping* records every transaction twice so arithmetic error or theft immediately produces a visible discrepancy.
    - *Independent replication / testing* rewards rivals for finding errors in a researcher's claim.
- **Discovered in Substance vs. Constructed in Form (The Bronze Age Assyrian Proof):**
  - When someone claims "markets, money, marriage, or borders are social constructs," ask: **Constructed in form, or constructed in substance?**
  - *Rule of Thumb:* If a functional arrangement reappears independently across unrelated cultures and millennia (or in prison camps and post-collapse black markets within weeks), its *substance* is a discovered technology solving a permanent constraint of reality, even though its local *form* is constructed.
  - *Historical Example — Old Assyrian Trade Network (c. 1900 BCE, Assur to Kanesh):* Four thousand years ago—millennia before Adam Smith or modern capitalism—Assyrian merchants carried tin and textiles 1,000 km by donkey caravan from northern Iraq (Assur) to central Anatolia (Kanesh). Over 20,000 surviving clay tablets document joint-stock partnerships capitalized in gold, profit-sharing formulas, early-withdrawal penalties, interest-bearing silver loans, commercial courts, notarized escrow at the Temple of Ashur, women managing textile production and lawsuits in Assur while husbands traveled, smuggling routes ("the narrow track") bypassing high tariffs, and trade volumes matching modern econometric gravity models. Markets, credit, and contract law were *discovered* responses to specialization and scarcity, not ideological inventions.

## 2. Human Nature: Dunbar's Number and Non-Modular Identity (Principle 2)
- **Dunbar's Number (Cognitive Bandwidth Limit on Relationships):**
  - Human social and emotional bandwidth is biologically capped in concentric layers: roughly **5** intimate support relationships, **15** trusted sympathy friends, **50** close friends, **150** stable personal relationships, **500** acquaintances, and **1,500** recognizable faces/names. This biological hardware limit makes universal equal care across millions or billions physically impossible.
- **Particularity: Identity Is Not Modular ("The Seagull and Termite Test" Against Rawls):**
  - Egalitarians (following **John Rawls's "Veil of Ignorance"**) argue: "You were lucky to be born in this family/country/body; you could have been born someone else in a failed state or in poverty, so all differences are unearned lottery prizes that must be redistributed."
  - *The Hidden Fallacy:* This assumes **modular identity**—that there was a featureless, pre-existing soul ("you") waiting in a cosmic lobby before birth, which was randomly inserted like a marble into a jar of bodies, families, and nations.
  - *The Seagull / Termite Test:* Ask whether "I could have been born a seagull" or "Given insect populations, I was statistically almost certain to be born a termite" is a meaningful statement. It is grammatical nonsense. A person is not a featureless soul issued a costume of genetics, sex, lineage, and culture; those particulars *constitute* the person. Subtract them and there is no "you" left over.
  - *Moral Consequence:* Your family's and nation's platform was not dealt by a cosmic casino; it was built and defended by a specific chain of ancestors. The proper response to an inherited platform is neither **guilt** (on the part of the fortunate) nor **entitlement** (on the part of outsiders), nor petitioning a non-existent cosmic referee, but **gratitude** to ancestors and **generational obligation** to maintain and pass the platform forward.

## 3. Goodness, Historical Collapses, and Beauty's Three Elements (Principle 3 & Virtues 3, 4, 6)
- **Historical Proofs that Civilization's Skills Vanish Without Maintenance:**
  - *Late Bronze Age Collapse (c. 1200 BCE):* Palace economies broke down; up to 90% of settlements in the Peloponnese were abandoned; the Linear B script was completely forgotten, leaving the Greek world illiterate for four hundred years.
  - *Collapse of Western Rome (5th–6th centuries CE):* The city of Rome shrank from ~1,000,000 inhabitants to a few tens of thousands; aqueducts broke and went unrepaired; coined money disappeared from daily trade; masonry and wheel-thrown pottery reverted to crude perishable forms.
- **Harm Reduction / Suffering Minimization Pushed to Its Logical Conclusion:**
  - If goodness is defined strictly as "the elimination of suffering and harm," then because agency allows bad choices, power allows injury, and childbirth brings beings into a world where they will inevitably suffer and die, the only state with zero suffering is a dead planet of **cold rocks and a hot sun** with no living minds. Anti-natalism is simply "harm reduction" carried honestly to its mathematical endpoint.
- **Parenting Motion: Hold, Then Loosen (Against Anxious Micromanagement):**
  - A characteristic failure mode of careful, wealthy parents is having only one child and micromanaging that child into fragility to prevent any scrape or failure.
  - *Operational rule:* A child who is never allowed to fail (a scraped knee, a lost contest, a poor grade earned by poor preparation) never develops real competence. The parental method is **hold, then loosen**: total protection in infancy, followed by expanding freedom and real consequences as competence grows, until the young adult can stand on his own.
- **Beauty's Triadic Structure (Order + Truth + Vitality) & Diagnostic Counter-Examples (Virtue 4):**
  - *Order without Vitality:* A tax spreadsheet is ordered and true, but sterile—nobody hangs it on a wall as art.
  - *Vitality without Order:* Raw noise or a riot has energy, but lacks intelligible proportion.
  - *Order and Vitality without Truth:* Totalitarian propaganda or a flattering lie—vivid and structured, but false to reality.
  - *Kintsugi / Repaired Vase Example:* A ceramic vase repaired with gold lacquer seams, or holding fresh cut flowers, joins structural order, honest history of damage and care (truth), and living continuation (vitality).
- **Outcomes Over Feelings (Social Promotion Example):**
  - *Example:* A school district adopts "social promotion"—passing every student to the next grade regardless of whether they can read or calculate, because holding a child back feels harsh and hurts their self-esteem today. The feeling is warm; the physical outcome five years later is an illiterate teenager locked out of productive trades.
  - *Scope note on feelings:* Within the household (Tier 2), a child's fear or a spouse's grief is real personal data requiring warmth and care; at the institutional/civic scale (Tier 4), policies must be judged strictly by measurable outcomes, because institutions cannot "love."

## 4. Tiers, Love, Politeness, Doomerism, and Parasitism (Principle 4 & Virtue 2)
- **The Family Bridge Example (How Tiered Priority Compounds Across Generations):**
  - Parents spend hundreds of hours clearing brambles and invasive brush from woods behind their house, suffering cuts and sweat, and build a sturdy bridge over a creek so their children can ride bikes across miles of trail.
  - To an atomized egalitarian (Rawls's veil), the child having a bridge while another child elsewhere does not looks like "unfair luck." In reality, the bridge only exists because tiered family ownership gave the parents a reason to invest hundreds of hours of unpaid labor—and the cleared brush simultaneously improves the surrounding neighborhood (Tier 3). Forbid parents from passing advantages to their own children, and no one clears the woods at all.
- **Kindness vs. Politeness (Why Unscoped Kindness Breeds Predators):**
  - **Kindness** is intimate, costly, patient forbearance (sitting with the sick, forgiving repeated stumbles). It works *only* inside inner tiers (family, close community) where members know one another, monitor behavior, and can cut off a chronic free-rider.
  - **Politeness** is low-cost, procedural civility among strangers (holding a door, waiting in a queue, speaking civilly).
  - *Selection Failure:* When a society mistakes intimate *kindness* for its public default toward unknown strangers, free-riders and predators move from host to host faster than reputation can track them. Unbounded public kindness systematically taxes the responsible to subsidize predators, selecting *against* kindness over time. **Rule: Kindness within family; politeness in society.**
- **Doomerism as a Self-Permission Structure & The Singapore 1965 Rebuke:**
  - *Diagnostic Test for Doomerism:* Across left ("the planet/system is doomed") and right ("the culture/west is finished"), doomerism always arrives at the exact same convenient conclusion for the speaker: *therefore, I am excused from the hard, tedious, twenty-year work of marriage, having children, maintaining property, and building institutions.* It is a permission slip for laziness and hedonism disguised as hard-bitten realism.
  - *Historical Rebuke — Singapore (1965):* Expelled from Malaysia in 1965 with zero natural resources, no hinterland, high unemployment, ethnic riots, and hostile neighbors, Singapore's leadership (Lee Kuan Yew) had every objective excuse for doomer surrender. Instead, they enforced strict procedural law, demanded excellence in schools and work, eradicated corruption, and built for the next generation—transforming a mudflat port into a first-world civilization within one lifetime.
- **Three Forms of Parasitism That Drain Civilizational Platforms:**
  1. **Ideological Parasitism:** Ideologies whose adherents have sub-replacement or near-zero fertility cannot survive biologically; they can only survive by capturing schools, media, and universities to convert *other people's children*. (Also includes **Grievance-Industrial Capture**: organizations funded to solve a social problem acquire a direct financial incentive to keep the problem unsolved so their budget and staff never terminate.)
  2. **Social Parasitism:** Distinct from the genuinely disabled, sick, young, or elderly (whom tiered responsibility rightly carries), the *social parasite* is the able-bodied person who is unwilling to work and lives by extracting the labor of others through crime or chronic welfare manipulation.
  3. **Technological Parasitism:** Whole cultures or groups that eagerly import and consume advanced technological outputs (antibiotics, jet aircraft, smartphones, electrical grids) while actively despising and rejecting the civilizational practices (truth-alignment, empirical testing, procedural symmetry, merit) required to invent and maintain them. *Test:* Could this culture have built what it uses, and if left alone with the machinery for two generations, would it still work?

## 5. Scoped Procedural Symmetry, Power, and the Intellectual Map (Principle 5)
- **Legitimate Power's Four Properties (The Parent-Child Canonical Model):**
  - Refusing to wield power ("no one should pick up the bat of power") simply hands the bat to the most ruthless predator. Power is necessary in every tier (parent, teacher, HOA president, police officer, judge, general).
  - Power is legitimate when it satisfies four conditions:
    1. **Justified by tiered responsibility** (exists to serve and protect those in the scope, as a parent's authority serves the child's upbringing).
    2. **Bounded by procedural symmetry** (the holder is subject to law and applies rules without arbitrary favoritism).
    3. **Temporary / Role-bound** (parental authority transfers as the child matures into a competent adult; public officials serve defined terms and can be removed).
    4. **Accountable** (outer tiers intervene if a parent abuses a child or an official takes a bribe).
- **Rule of Law vs. Rule by Bureaucrats, Over-Criminalization, and the Violent Few:**
  - *Rule by Bureaucrats:* Shifting administrative guidance that changes quarterly without fixed statute is not Rule of Law.
  - *Knowability of Law:* Because humans are limited (Principle 2), laws must be concise and knowable by an ordinary citizen. A legal code of tens of thousands of pages turns every citizen into a technical felon and gives prosecutors arbitrary power to target political enemies (**selective enforcement**).
  - *Leniency as Reallocated Violence:* Releasing a repeat violent predator does not reduce violence in the world; it transfers the next assault from the guilty predator onto an innocent future victim. Moreover, violent crime is Pareto-concentrated: a tiny fraction of repeat offenders commits the vast majority of violent crime; incapacitating that fraction transforms an entire city's freedom and trust.
- **Asymmetric Polities and Diaspora Mobilization (Why Borders Must Filter):**
  - Not all foreign states are constitutional republics where citizens are private individuals. Some states are **mobilization regimes** (or politico-religious orders) that treat their diaspora—regardless of citizenship—as instruments of the home state, compelling their speech, espionage, or political action through threats to relatives back home. Treating agents of a mobilization regime with naive unscoped openness allows a hostile regime to use a free society's own procedures to subvert it.
  - **Selective Adoption Model for Immigration:** Naturalization is like adopting an adult into a family household. It works only with: (1) strict pre-entry filtering for demonstrated competence, self-sufficiency, and cultural compatibility; (2) a multi-year probationary period where net fiscal contribution, language mastery, and law-abiding conduct are verified (with welfare dependence or crime terminating probation); (3) revocation/denaturalization for fraud or subversion; and (4) strict numerical caps so the host population's median habits and cohesion are never overwhelmed.
- **The Compassion Weapon & False "Virtues" (Tolerance, Diversity, Harm Reduction):**
  - **The Compassionate Delusion (Trojan Horse for Outcome Symmetry):** Because citizens would reject an open demand to abolish equal laws, outcome symmetry is smuggled in as "compassion"—excusing theft, disorder, or border-jumping by pointing to the offender's hardship.
  - **Refusing the Compassion Weapon in One Sentence:** When an adversary demands you open a border, excuse a predator, or surrender an institution "for the children / the vulnerable," answer: *"My compassion for my own children and community is precisely why I refuse."*
  - **Why "Tolerance" Is Not a Foundational Virtue (Paradox of Tolerance & Herbert Marcuse):** Tolerance is a healthy *secondary practice* within a shared civilizational frame, not a foundational virtue. If tolerance is made the highest good, the most intolerant group wins automatically. In 1965, **Herbert Marcuse** explicitly formulated **"Repressive Tolerance"**—demanding tolerance for left-wing revolutionary movements while withdrawing tolerance from conservatives—turning "tolerance" into a one-way weapon.
  - **Why "Diversity" Is Not a Foundational Virtue:** Complementary diversity of skills and trades (butcher, baker, engineer, physician) strengthens a city; diversity of foundational premises (whether truth exists, whether contracts bind, whether violence is legal) tears a polity apart. Unity logically requires **exclusion** of what contradicts the unifying standard (a choir unified in one key excludes singing off-key).
