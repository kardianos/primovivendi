## Chapter 13: Historical Case Studies: Singapore, Russia, Iran, and Tactical Alliances

Let us now move from the examination of philosophical systems to the **historical record of nations**.

When we evaluate modern history under the Five Principles, we must apply **Three Long-Horizon Criteria**:

1. **The Generational Criterion (70–100 Years):** Never evaluate a policy by its immediate sentiment during the first eighteen months of borrowed expenditure or revolutionary enthusiasm. Evaluate it by the condition of the country when the grandchildren inherit the resulting institutions and balance sheet seventy years later.
2. **The Scale Criterion (Why a Monastery Cannot Be Scaled Linearly to a Nation of Fifty Million):** A voluntary commune, monastery, or early kibbutz of eighty selected members can sometimes operate for a few decades on informal communal sharing (Tier 2/3 *kindness*), because every member dines in the same hall and idlers can be corrected or expelled in person. If one attempts to scale that communal arrangement linearly to a nation of fifty million strangers without **Principle 5 (Scoped Procedural Symmetry)** and private property (**Principle 4**), it collapses into bureaucratic coercion and mass deprivation.
3. **The Interconnect Criterion:** The Five Principles are not an arbitrary collection from which one may retain two principles and discard three. Truth (Principle 1), realist human nature (Principle 2), keeping and building (Principle 3), tiered family and defense (Principle 4), and symmetrical courts (Principle 5) depend structurally upon one another like the stones of a masonry arch.

Three twentieth-century historical cases demonstrate how these principles operate in practice: **Singapore (1965–1990)**, **Russia (1907–1933)**, and **Iran (1978–1983)**.

### 1. Singapore (1965–1990): Statecraft Under Resource Scarcity

If a fatalist or an ideological determinist had sought one territory in the world in **August 1965** that appeared destined for poverty, ethnic warfare, and foreign conquest, he would have selected the island of **Singapore**.

The physical and political facts on **August 9, 1965**, the morning Malaysia expelled Singapore from the Federation and Prime Minister **Lee Kuan Yew** addressed the nation, were severe:

* Two million people crowded onto a tropical island of 224 square miles of mudflats and marshland, with **no oil, no coal, no iron ore, no agricultural hinterland, and an insufficient domestic supply of fresh drinking water** (its water arrived through pipelines from Johor in Malaysia, which could sever the supply at will).
* A population deeply divided by ethnicity, language, and religion (approximately 75% Chinese speaking several mutually unintelligible dialects, 14% Malay Muslim, and 8% Indian Tamil and others), with violent **Chinese-Malay race riots** having killed dozens in the streets in 1964.
* Militant Communist trade unions paralyzing the docks with strikes, supported by revolutionary movements across Southeast Asia, while Indonesia's Sukarno was conducting undeclared military sabotage (*Konfrontasi*) against the island.
* Within three years, Britain announced that it was withdrawing its naval base and military garrison, which had accounted for **twenty percent of Singapore's entire economy** and all of its external defense.

Every prevailing academic theory of the 1960s, including "Dependency Theory," socialist state ownership, post-colonial grievance politics, and ethnic majoritarianism, predicted that Singapore would become an impoverished, disorder-ridden dependency.

Instead, within a single generation, Singapore rose from poverty to achieve one of the highest per-capita incomes, lowest crime rates, highest educational standards, longest life expectancies, and busiest ports and financial centers in the world.

**How was this achieved?** Lee Kuan Yew and his founding cabinet (Goh Keng Swee, S. Rajaratnam, Hon Sui Sen) rejected prevailing ideological illusions and structured their policies around the **Five Common Principles of Reality**:

#### 1. Alignment With Principle 1 (Reality Is Real and Consequential) and Virtue 2 (Ownership)

Days after independence in 1965, Lee Kuan Yew stated the foundational premise of Singapore's existence to its citizens:

> ***"The world does not owe us a living. We cannot live by the begging bowl."***

Lee refused to indulge in political excuses: he devoted **no time** to lamenting British colonial rule, demanding foreign reparations, or blaming external structural forces. He addressed physical reality directly: two million people on a resource-poor island had to produce goods and services so reliable, sound, and economical that the rest of the world would choose to trade, bank, and manufacture in Singapore rather than elsewhere.

#### 2. Alignment With Principle 2 (Particularity and Limits) and Virtue 3 (Excellence)

While Western universities in the late 1960s increasingly embraced egalitarian leveling, Lee Kuan Yew spoke directly about human particularity and the necessity of cultivating high character and technical excellence. In 1966–1967, addressing school principals and students, Lee compared civic character to metallurgy:

* One cannot construct a durable edifice out of soft pig-iron; a society must temper its citizens, both in physical stamina and moral character, into ***"steel."***
* Addressing the reality of human cognitive and executive variation (**Principle 2**), Lee noted that in any population, the most capable, disciplined, and public-spirited five to ten percent of citizens act as ***"the yeast, the ferment, the catalyst"*** that enables the entire society to advance. If a nation persecutes, drives out, or levels down its most capable engineers, administrators, and entrepreneurs in the name of envy, the remaining ninety percent do not become wealthier; they sink into unemployment and poverty. Therefore, Singapore established rigorously meritocratic schools, technical institutes, and civil service examinations in which the son of a manual laborer who scored at the top of the mathematics examination received a scholarship alongside the son of a banker.

#### 3. Alignment With Principle 3 and Principle 4 (Self-Anchored Ownership Over Unconditional Welfare Subsidies)

How did Singapore provide for housing, healthcare, and retirement without adopting unconditional welfare transfer systems that subsidize family breakdown and idleness?

* Through the **Central Provident Fund (CPF)** and the **Housing and Development Board (HDB) Home-Ownership Scheme**. Instead of taxing working citizens to fund an anonymous pool from which free riders could draw benefits without working, Singapore required every working citizen and his employer to contribute into **that citizen's own self-anchored, individual account** for housing, medical care, and retirement.
* Furthermore, Lee insisted that families **own** their apartments rather than renting them as state tenants, because he understood human nature (**Principle 2 and Principle 4**): when riots broke out, tenants who owned nothing left their apartments neglected and watched the street burn; **homeowners who held equity in their apartments maintained the corridors, guarded the buildings, and were prepared to serve in the armed forces to defend the country in which their family's home stood.**

#### 4. Alignment With Principle 5 (Scoped Procedural Symmetry Across Ethnic Groups)

In a region where neighboring states enacted ethnic quota systems that barred minority Chinese and Indians from universities and civil service posts, or where rulers dismissed judges when a court ruled against the government, Lee Kuan Yew enforced **Scoped Procedural Symmetry**:

* Although ethnic Chinese comprised three-quarters of Singapore's electorate, Lee refused to make Mandarin the sole dominant state language over the Malay and Indian minorities. Instead, he made **English** the neutral, symmetrical working language of the courts, universities, and commerce, placing every citizen under the same procedural standard while connecting Singapore directly to international science and trade, while each child also studied his mother tongue (Mandarin, Malay, or Tamil) in school.
* Within the courtroom and marketplace, English common-law contract and property rules were enforced with strict impartiality, and the **Corrupt Practices Investigation Bureau (CPIB)** prosecuted even cabinet ministers who accepted bribes, making Singapore one of the least corrupt jurisdictions in the world.

#### 5. Alignment With Principle 4's Final Clause ("Enemies Are Real")

When Britain announced its military withdrawal, Lee did not rely upon diplomatic appeals to the United Nations for security. Requesting military advisors from Israel (the only country willing to train Singapore's nascent armed forces in 1965), Singapore built a universal male conscription defense force (**National Service**) on what Lee termed the **"Poisonous Shrimp" doctrine** (and later the "Dolphin" doctrine): a small island cannot conquer a large neighbor, **but it can make itself so well-armed, disciplined, and formidable that any aggressor attempting to conquer it will suffer prohibitive losses, remaining, in Lee's phrase, *"not a digestible morsel."***

#### The Analytical Caveat on Singapore: Adopt the Principles, Guard Against Demographic Decline

When we study Singapore under the Five Principles, we must apply the same impartial judgment we apply elsewhere:

1. **Adopt the Foundational Principles, Not Every Emergency Regulation:** A large continental republic does not need, and should not copy, every municipal regulation or restriction that a small island city-state adopted during a 1960s communist insurgency. What any nation *can* and *should* adopt are Singapore's alignment with reality, meritocratic standards, self-anchored ownership accounts, public order, swift punishment of violent crime and corruption, and symmetrical commercial courts.
2. **Where Modern Singapore Is Vulnerable:** This vulnerability appears in **Virtue 6 (Have children)** in twenty-first-century Singapore. Because modern Singapore became a high-cost, high-density urban economy in which young adults delay marriage and childbearing for corporate careers, its Total Fertility Rate has fallen below **1.0 child per woman**. Even Lee Kuan Yew in his final years warned that this demographic decline was the single greatest threat to Singapore's survival, demonstrating once again that **The Interconnect Criterion** admits no exceptions: a civilization must maintain **all** Five Principles and **all** Seven Virtues, or the seventy-year replacement horizon overtakes even the most capable material builders.

### 2. Russia (1907–1933): From Stolypin's Farms and Durnovo's Warning to Lenin's Ruin

Now turn from an island that rose by embracing the Five Principles to a vast empire that destroyed itself by repudiating them: **Russia between 1907 and 1933**.

It is frequently assumed that Imperial Russia before 1917 was a static, backward society destined inevitably for Bolshevik communism. The actual historical record presents a far more instructive account regarding **Principle 3 (Keeping and Building)**, **Principle 4 (Self-Anchored Family Property)**, and the consequences when a state disregards strategic reality.

#### 1. Pyotr Stolypin's Property Reforms (1906–1911): Relying Upon "The Sturdy and the Strong"

When Russian peasants were emancipated from serfdom in 1861, most were not granted individual family title to their land. Instead, land was held collectively by the village commune (the ***mir*** or ***obshchina***), which periodically **redistributed strips of land** among households by headcount and forbade any peasant farmer from consolidating his strips, fencing his field, or adopting modern crop rotation without the unanimous vote of the village assembly.

* The incentives of the communal *mir* under **Principle 2** were counterproductive: why would an industrious peasant spend three years draining a marsh, hauling manure, and planting fruit trees on Strip #4 if the village commune would confiscate Strip #4 at the next redistribution and assign it to an negligent neighbor? The result was chronic agricultural stagnation, strip-farming inefficiency, and rural grievance.
* In 1906, Tsar Nicholas II appointed **Pyotr Stolypin**, a capable and resolute provincial governor, as Prime Minister. Stolypin diagnosed the fundamental problem immediately: Russia's peasants were radicalized and impoverished because **they had never been permitted to become self-anchored property owners (Principle 4 and Virtue 2).**
* Between 1906 and 1911, Stolypin enacted his landmark agrarian reforms, granting every peasant household the legal right to exit the leveling commune and claim **permanent, hereditary private ownership** of a consolidated family farmstead (*khutor* or *otrub*), supported by a Peasant Land Bank and voluntary resettlement to fertile lands in Siberia.
* In a speech to the State Duma on **May 10, 1907**, defending private family property against socialist levelers who demanded that the state confiscate all land, Stolypin explained that communal leveling cripples the most capable producers to appease the least capable, whereas his reform placed its reliance not upon *"the drunken and the weak,"* but upon ***"the sturdy and the strong."*** Grant an industrious peasant clear title to his own land so that his labor feeds his own children, Stolypin argued, and *"give the state twenty years of internal and external peace, and you will not recognize Russia."*
* What occurred in practice over the next seven years (1907–1914)? **The policy succeeded.** Millions of peasant households applied for private title; grain harvests, railway freight, cooperative banks, and industrial output expanded rapidly. Revolutionary terrorists attempted ten times to assassinate Stolypin, finally shooting him to death at the Kiev Opera House in September 1911, because **Lenin and the socialist revolutionaries recognized that if Stolypin's reform succeeded in transforming Russia's peasants into prosperous, self-anchored family landowners, the peasantry would reject Communist revolution.**

#### 2. Pyotr Durnovo's Memorandum (February 1914)

Stolypin had asked for *"twenty years of internal and external peace"* for the new property order to take firm root. Russia received only seven.

In **February 1914**, five months before the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand triggered the First World War, the former Interior Minister **Pyotr Nikolayevich Durnovo** submitted to Tsar Nicholas II a confidential thirty-page state memorandum that remains one of the most accurate strategic forecasts in modern history.

Applying strict **Principle 1** realism, Durnovo warned the Tsar:

1. Russia had no strategic interest to gain from a general war with Germany that could outweigh the severe strain on Russia's incomplete railways, munitions factories, and rural economy.
2. In the event of a prolonged, exhausting war accompanied by military reverses at the front, Russia's liberal opposition, the educated parliamentary intelligentsia, would use wartime hardships to overthrow the monarchy, **assuming that they could govern Russia as a peaceful parliamentary democracy.**
3. **Durnovo then stated precisely what would follow:** because Russia's educated liberal politicians possessed no executive experience and no support among the peasantry, while the socialist agitators preached simple slogans of total land and factory seizure to a population in which Stolypin's property reforms were still only halfway complete, the liberal government would be overthrown within months by ruthless extremists, and ***"Russia will be flung into hopeless anarchy, the issue of which cannot be even foreseen."***

The Tsar left Durnovo's memorandum unheeded, entered the war in August 1914, and every paragraph of Durnovo's forecast was verified by events between February and October 1917.

#### 3. Lenin's April Theses (1917), War Communism, and the War Against Reality

When **Vladimir Lenin** arrived at Petrograd's Finland Station in April 1917 and issued his **April Theses**, followed by the Bolshevik seizure of power in October, he systematically inverted all Five Principles of Reality:

* **Against Principle 1:** Objective truth and scientific impartiality were abolished in favor of *partiynost* ("Party-mindedness").
* **Against Principle 3 and Principle 4:** Under **War Communism (1918–1921)** and later Stalin's **Forced Collectivization (1929–1933)**, the Bolsheviks waged war against the very class of competent, industrious family farmers (*kulaks*) whom Stolypin had enabled. The application of the term ***kulak*** was systematically destructive: in practice, any peasant who kept his barn roof repaired, owned two cows or a metal plow, and harvested more grain per acre than his negligent neighbor was designated a "class enemy," stripped of his home and tools, and shot or deported in unheated railcars to the Arctic wilderness.
* **Against Principle 5:** Independent courts and symmetrical statutes were replaced by the Cheka's **"Revolutionary Conscience,"** which explicitly instructed tribunals not to look for evidence of what offense a defendant had committed, but first to ask **what class he belonged to, what his parents were, and what his education was.**
* **The Consequence in Reality:** Once the competent farmers were executed or deported and the remaining peasants were forced back into state collectives where they owned nothing they produced, agricultural production collapsed and **more than ten million human beings starved to death** across the Volga, Ukraine (the *Holodomor*), and Kazakhstan in one of the most fertile grain-producing regions in the world. Physical reality is indifferent to revolutionary slogans; destroy the producers and abolish ownership, and the granaries empty.

### 3. Iran (1978–1983) and the Mechanics of Modern Red-Green Tactical Alliances

A third historical case study, **Iran between 1978 and 1983**, illuminates one of the most instructive phenomena in modern politics: **How can two movements whose positive doctrines directly contradict each other cooperate closely to overthrow an existing civilization?**

#### 1. The Shah's Warning: "The Unholy Alliance of Red and Black"

In the 1970s, Iran under **Mohammad Reza Shah Pahlavi** was rapidly industrializing, building modern universities, expanding secular courts, and educating women. While the regime possessed real defects and faced domestic opposition, **two incompatible forces combined in 1978 to overthrow the Iranian state**:

1. **The "Red" Faction (Marxist-Leninists and Secular Left-Wing Intellectuals):** The Communist **Tudeh Party**, the Marxist-Leninist *Fedaiyan-e Khalq*, the Islamic-Marxist *Mujahedin-e Khalq* (MEK), and Western-educated progressive university students who sought a socialist workers' republic, gender egalitarianism, and the overthrow of Western capitalism.
2. **The "Black/Green" Faction (Khomeini's Theocratic Islamists):** **Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini** and his militant clerical network, whose explicit goal was to abolish secular law, impose strict *sharia* gender segregation and veiling, execute apostates and those accused of "corruption in the land" (*mofsed-e-filarz*), and establish absolute rule by Islamic jurists (*Velayat-e Faqih*).

In doctrine, a Marxist feminist student in Tehran and a theocratic cleric disagreed on almost every positive question of how human beings should live. **Why, then, did they march together in the streets of Tehran throughout 1978?**

The Shah identified their coalition accurately as ***"the unholy alliance of Red and Black."***

The example of **Two Demolition Crews Pulling Down One Stone Wall** illustrates how such an alliance operates, and why it ends in the destruction of the weaker partner:

> **Two Crews Pulling Down One Wall:** Around a prosperous city stands a high stone constitutional wall that protects the homes, shops, and courts inside. Outside that wall stand two rival factions: Crew A wants to burn the city down and build a Communist barracks on the ruins; Crew B wants to burn the city down and build a theocratic fortress on the ruins.
>
> Neither Crew A nor Crew B possesses sufficient strength to batter down the stone wall by itself. Therefore, on Monday morning, the two crews tie their ropes to the same timber ram and pull together, chanting the same negative slogan: *"Down with the Wall."*
>
> Inside the city, naive observers look out the window and say: *"How absurd to claim Crew A and Crew B are working together. Crew A is atheist and progressive; Crew B is fundamentalist and patriarchal. They could not possibly be allies."*
>
> Those naive observers confuse **positive program** with **negative demolition**. While the wall remains standing, two incompatible wrecking crews share a **complete tactical interest in pulling down the constitutional structure that restrains them both.**

And what occurred in Iran on the morning *after* the wall came down in February 1979?

The Tudeh Communist leaders and secular progressive professors expected to share cabinet ministries in a new revolutionary government. Instead, between **1979 and 1983**, Khomeini's clerics, who controlled the mosques, the street militias (*Komitehs*), and the new Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, systematically closed every independent newspaper, purged the universities in the "Islamic Cultural Revolution," forced women back under the mandatory veil on pain of flogging, outlawed the Tudeh Party and leftist organizations, and **arrested, tortured, and executed thousands of their former Marxist and student allies inside Evin Prison.**

When two anti-procedural factions combine to overthrow a constitutional order, they do not coexist peacefully once the common obstacle is removed; **the stronger and more ruthless faction immediately destroys the weaker faction.**

#### 2. The Five Mechanics of Modern Red-Green Coordination in the West

Today, in the streets, universities, municipal councils, and non-governmental organizations of Western constitutional democracies, the same **Red-Green Tactical Alliance** operates (radical progressive and Marxist activists cooperating with political Islamist organizations):

* Radical progressive activists who denounce traditional Christians over social doctrines simultaneously march alongside Islamist groups whose classical legal doctrine prescribes severe punishment for homosexuality and legal subordination for women and religious minorities because **both share the immediate tactical goal of subverting and paralyzing the host Western constitutional order.**

This Red-Green alliance operates within an open society through **five operational mechanics**:

1. **The Shield and the Sword:** The Islamist movement supplies street intimidation, demographic mobilization, and sectarian solidarity (**the Sword**), while the progressive academic, media, and legal apparatus supplies ideological cover (**the Shield**), immediately branding any citizen, police officer, or magistrate who attempts to enforce symmetrical law against Islamist intimidation as a *"racist"* or *"Islamophobe."*
2. **Dual-Code Speech:** Speaking one language in English to progressive media outlets (using the vocabulary of "civil rights," "inclusion," and "minority protection" to demand institutional concessions) while speaking a different language internally in sectarian assemblies (the language of conquest, demographic replacement, and religious supremacy).
3. **Humiliation Politics in Public Space:** Deliberately blocking major highways, bridges, train stations, and civic monuments, not to persuade fellow citizens, but to **visibly demonstrate that the host police and courts are too timid to enforce the law against them**, thereby demoralizing law-abiding citizens and establishing dominance over public space.
4. **Speech Sacralization and Blasphemy Shields:** Conditioning a free society to accept that while its own heritage, constitution, and majority religion may be disparaged daily at public expense, criticizing the allied faction's dogma constitutes "hate speech" or "incitement" that must be censored by the state or punished by street violence.
5. **Institutional Entryism:** Using the host society's own "diversity and inclusion" mandates to place factional activists inside school boards, police oversight panels, human-rights commissions, and prosecutor offices, where they enforce **procedural asymmetry** (Marcuse's *Repressive Tolerance* in action).

**How does a healthy civilization defeat this coordination?** Not by abandoning its own principles or adopting right-wing authoritarianism, **but by enforcing Principle 5 (Scoped Procedural Symmetry) with unwavering consistency**:

* No tolerance for highway blockades, street intimidation, or political violence, regardless of which banner the offender carries.
* No blasphemy shields or speech codes: one symmetrical rule of free speech and public order for every citizen.
* No public funding or legal jurisdiction for any organization that operates as an instrument of a foreign state or seeks to replace constitutional symmetry with sectarian law.
