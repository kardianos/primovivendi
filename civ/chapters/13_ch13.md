## Chapter 13: History Graded by Reality — Singapore, Russia, Iran, and Tactical Alliances

Let us now move from the library of philosophers to the **laboratory of nations**.

When we look at modern history through the Five Principles, we should always apply **Three Long-Horizon Lenses**:

1. **The Generational Lens (70–100 Years):** Never grade a policy by how it feels in the first eighteen months of borrowed money or revolutionary euphoria. Grade it by what the country looks like when the grandchildren inherit the balance sheet seventy years later.
2. **The Scale Lens (Why a Monastery Cannot Be Scaled Linearly to a Nation of Fifty Million):** A voluntary commune, monastery, or early kibbutz of eighty hand-picked zealots can sometimes run for a few decades on informal "share everything equally" sentiment (Tier 2/3 *kindness*), because everyone eats at one table and lazy slackers can be shamed or expelled in person. Try to scale that commune ruleset linearly to a nation of fifty million strangers without **Principle 5 (Scoped Procedural Symmetry)** and private property (**Principle 4**), and it instantly collapses into bureaucratic tyranny and mass starvation.
3. **The Interconnect Lens:** The Five Principles are not a buffet where you can pick two and throw away three. Truth (Principle 1), realist human nature (Principle 2), keeping and building (Principle 3), tiered family and defense (Principle 4), and symmetrical courts (Principle 5) lock together like the arch stones of a bridge.

Let us watch three dramatic twentieth-century historical experiments that prove how these principles operate in real time: **Singapore (1965–1990)**, **Russia (1907–1933)**, and **Iran (1978–1983)**.

### 1. Singapore (1965–1990): How Lee Kuan Yew Built a First-World Oasis on Mudflats

If a doomer or an ideological determinist wanted to pick one spot on earth in **August 1965** that appeared mathematically doomed to poverty, race war, and foreign conquest, he would have pointed to the tiny island of **Singapore**.

Look at the physical facts on **August 9, 1965**, the morning Malaysia expelled Singapore from the Federation and Prime Minister **Lee Kuan Yew** wept on television:

* Two million people crowded onto a tropical island of 224 square miles of mudflats and swamp—with **zero oil, zero coal, zero iron ore, zero hinterland farms, and not even enough fresh drinking water** (its water came through pipes from Johor in Malaysia, which could turn off the tap at will).
* A population deeply divided by ethnicity, language, and religion (roughly 75% Chinese speaking half a dozen mutually unintelligible dialects, 14% Malay Muslim, 8% Indian Tamil and others), with bloody **Chinese-Malay race riots** having just killed dozens in the streets in 1964.
* Militant Communist trade unions paralyzing the docks with strikes, backed by revolutionary movements across Southeast Asia, while Indonesia's Sukarno was waging undeclared military sabotage (*Konfrontasi*) against the island.
* Within three years, Britain announced it was pulling out its naval base and military garrison—which had accounted for **twenty percent of Singapore's entire economy** and all of its military defense.

Every fashionable academic theory of the 1960s—"Dependency Theory," socialist state-ownership, third-world grievance politics, or ethnic majoritarianism—predicted that Singapore would end up a impoverished, riot-torn slum.

Instead, within a single generation, Singapore rose from third-world poverty to have one of the highest per-capita incomes, lowest crime rates, highest educational achievements, longest life expectancies, and busiest ports and financial centers on the planet.

**How?** Because Lee Kuan Yew and his founding team (Goh Keng Swee, S. Rajaratnam, Hon Sui Sen) rejected fashionable illusions and engineered every single policy around the **Five Common Principles of Reality**:

#### 1. Alignment With Principle 1 (Reality Is Real and Consequential) and Virtue 2 (Ownership)

Days after independence in 1965, Lee Kuan Yew stood before his citizens and stated the foundational premise of Singapore's existence:

> ***"The world does not owe us a living. We cannot live by the begging bowl."***

Notice what Lee refused to do: he spent **zero hours** crying about British colonialism, demanding foreign reparations, or blaming global structural forces. He looked straight at physical reality: two million people on a rock must produce goods and services so reliable, honest, and excellent that the entire world chooses to trade, bank, and manufacture in Singapore rather than anywhere else.

#### 2. Alignment With Principle 2 (Particularity and Limits) and Virtue 3 (Excellence)

While Western universities in the late 1960s were falling in love with Rousseau's Blank Slate and egalitarian leveling, Lee Kuan Yew spoke bluntly about human particularity and the necessity of cultivating high-character excellence. In 1966–1967, speaking to school principals and students, Lee compared human quality to metallurgy:

* You cannot build a skyscraper out of soft pig-iron; a society must forge its citizens—both in physical stamina and moral character—into ***"steel."***
* And speaking of the reality of human cognitive and executive distribution (**Principle 2**), Lee pointed out that in any population, the top five to ten percent of capable, disciplined, public-spirited builders act as ***"the yeast, the ferment, the catalyst"*** that allows the entire loaf of society to rise. If a nation persecutes, drives out, or levels down its most capable engineers, administrators, and entrepreneurs in the name of envy, the remaining ninety percent do not get richer—they sink into unemployment and misery! Therefore, Singapore built rigorously meritocratic schools, technical institutes, and civil service exams where the son of a trishaw driver who scored at the top of the math exam won a scholarship alongside the son of a banker.

#### 3. Alignment With Principle 3 & Principle 4 (Self-Anchored Ownership Over Bent Cushions)

How did Singapore handle housing, healthcare, and retirement without falling into the Western welfare-state trap that subsidizes family breakdown and idleness?

* Through the **Central Provident Fund (CPF)** and the **Housing and Development Board (HDB) Home-Ownership Scheme**. Instead of taxing working people to fund an anonymous pool where free riders could draw benefits without working, Singapore required every working citizen and his employer to contribute into **that citizen's own self-anchored, personal account** for housing, medical care, and retirement.
* Even more brilliantly, Lee insisted that families **own** their apartments rather than renting them as state tenants. Why? Because Lee observed human nature (**Principle 2 and Principle 4**): when riots broke out, tenants who owned nothing left their apartments dirty and watched the street burn; **homeowners who owned the equity in their apartment swept the corridor, guarded the building, and were ready to fight and die in the army to defend the country where their family's home stood!**

#### 4. Alignment With Principle 5 (Scoped Procedural Symmetry Across Races)

In a region where neighboring states enacted ethnic affirmative-action quotas that barred minority Chinese and Indians from universities and civil service posts, or where rulers detained their own Chief Justices when a court ruled against the regime, Lee Kuan Yew enforced **Scoped Procedural Symmetry**:

* Although ethnic Chinese made up three-quarters of Singapore's voters, Lee refused to make Mandarin the sole dominant state language over the Malay and Indian minorities. Instead, he made **English** the neutral, symmetrical working language of the courts, universities, and commerce—putting every citizen at the same starting line while connecting Singapore directly to global science and trade—while each child also learned his mother tongue (Mandarin, Malay, or Tamil) in school.
* Inside the courtroom and marketplace, English common-law contract and property rules were enforced with legendary speed and impartiality, and the **Corrupt Practices Investigation Bureau (CPIB)** prosecuted even cabinet ministers who took a bribe—making Singapore one of the least corrupt jurisdictions on earth.

#### 5. Alignment With Principle 4's Final Clause ("Enemies Are Real")

When Britain announced its military withdrawal, Lee did not go to the United Nations to plead for a "right to exist." Reaching out to Israel (the only country willing to send military advisors to train Singapore's fledgling army in 1965), Singapore built a universal male conscription defense force (**National Service**) on what Lee called the **"Poisonous Shrimp" doctrine** (and later the "Dolphin" doctrine): a small island cannot conquer a giant neighbor, **but it can make itself so heavily armed, disciplined, and lethal that any predator who tries to swallow it will die of the bite—*"not a digestible morsel."***

#### The Engineering Caveat on Singapore: Copy the Premises, Guard the Seventy-Year Cliff

When we study Singapore under the Five Principles, we must apply the same honest engineering judgment we apply everywhere else:

1. **Copy the Root Premises, Not Every Siege Tactic:** A vast continental republic does not need—and should not copy—every municipal regulation or speech restriction that a tiny island city-state adopted during a 1960s communist insurgency. What any nation *can* and *must* copy are Singapore's alignment with reality, meritocratic excellence, self-anchored ownership accounts, clean streets, swift punishment of violent crime and corruption, and symmetrical commercial courts.
2. **Notice Where Modern Singapore Is Now Vulnerable:** Look at **Virtue 6 (Have children)** in twenty-first-century Singapore. Because modern Singapore became an intensely high-cost, high-pressure urban apartment economy where young adults delay marriage and childbearing for corporate careers, its Total Fertility Rate has dropped below **1.0 child per woman**. Even Lee Kuan Yew in his final years warned that this demographic cliff was the single greatest existential threat to Singapore's survival—proving once again that **The Interconnect Lens** holds no exceptions: a civilization must keep **all** Five Principles and **all** Seven Virtues in repair, or the Seventy-Year Cliff catches up with even the finest material builders.

### 2. Russia (1907–1933): From Stolypin's Farms and Durnovo's Warning to Lenin's Ruin

Now turn from an island that rose by embracing the Five Principles to a vast empire that tore itself apart by destroying them: **Russia between 1907 and 1933**.

Many people lazily imagine that Imperial Russia before 1917 was a static, hopeless medieval bog doomed inevitably to Bolshevik communism. The actual historical record tells a far more dramatic and instructive story about **Principle 3 (Keeping and Building)**, **Principle 4 (Self-Anchored Family Property)**, and what happens when a state ignores strategic reality.

#### 1. Pyotr Stolypin's Property Reforms (1906–1911): Betting on "The Sober and the Strong"

When Russian peasants were freed from serfdom in 1861, most were not given individual family title to their land. Instead, land was held collectively by the village commune (the ***mir*** or ***obshchina***), which periodically **redistributed strips of land** among households by headcount and forbade any peasant farmer from consolidating his strips, fencing his field, or adopting modern crop rotation without the unanimous vote of the village assembly.

* Look at the incentives of the communal *mir* under **Principle 2**: why would a hardworking peasant spend three years draining a marsh, hauling manure, and planting fruit trees on Strip #4 if the village commune was going to confiscate Strip #4 at the next redistribution and hand it to the village drunkard? The result was chronic agricultural stagnation, strip-farming inefficiency, and rural resentment.
* In 1906, Tsar Nicholas II appointed **Pyotr Stolypin**—a fearless, clear-eyed provincial governor—as Prime Minister. Stolypin diagnosed the root problem immediately: Russia's peasants were radicalized and poor because **they had never been allowed to become self-anchored property owners (Principle 4 and Virtue 2)!**
* Between 1906 and 1911, Stolypin enacted his landmark agrarian reforms, giving every peasant household the legal right to exit the leveling commune and claim **permanent, hereditary private ownership** of a consolidated family farmstead (*khutor* or *otrub*), backed by a Peasant Land Bank and voluntary resettlement to fertile lands in Siberia.
* In a famous speech to the State Duma on **May 10, 1907**, defending private family property against socialist levelers who wanted the state to confiscate all land, Stolypin explained that communal leveling cripples the best builders to appease the worst, whereas his reform placed its wager not on *"the drunken and the weak,"* but on ***"the sturdy and the strong"***. Give a hardworking peasant clear title to his own land so his sweat feeds his own children, Stolypin argued, and *"give the state twenty years of internal and external peace, and you will not recognize Russia."*
* What happened in reality over the next seven years (1907–1914)? **It worked!** Millions of peasant households applied for private title; grain harvests, railway freight, cooperative banks, and industrial output surged at some of the fastest rates in the world. Why did revolutionary terrorists try ten times to murder Stolypin—finally shooting him to death at the Kiev Opera House in September 1911? Because **Lenin and the socialist revolutionaries knew that if Stolypin's reform succeeded in turning Russia's peasants into prosperous, self-anchored family landowners, no peasant would ever join a Communist revolution!**

#### 2. Pyotr Durnovo's Prophetic Memorandum (February 1914)

Stolypin had asked for *"twenty years of internal and external peace"* for the new property order to take deep root. He got only seven.

In **February 1914**—five months before the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand triggered World War I—the former Interior Minister **Pyotr Nikolayevich Durnovo** handed Tsar Nicholas II a confidential thirty-page state paper that remains one of the most astonishingly accurate strategic forecasts in human history.

Applying cold **Principle 1** realism, Durnovo warned the Tsar:

1. Russia had zero strategic interest to gain from a general war with Germany that would outweigh the catastrophic strain on Russia's incomplete railways, munitions factories, and rural economy.
2. In the event of a prolonged, exhausting war with reverses at the front, Russia's liberal opposition—the educated parliamentary intelligentsia—would use wartime hardships to topple the monarchy, **imagining that they could govern Russia as a peaceful parliamentary democracy.**
3. **And then Durnovo wrote down exactly what would happen next:** because Russia's educated liberal politicians had zero executive experience and no roots among the peasantry, while the socialist agitators preached simple, intoxicating slogans of total land and factory seizure to a population where Stolypin's property reforms were still only halfway complete, the liberal government would be swept away within months by ruthless extremists, and ***"Russia will be flung into hopeless anarchy, the issue of which cannot be even foreseen."***

The Tsar left Durnovo's memorandum unread in a drawer, marched into war in August 1914, and every single paragraph of Durnovo's forecast came true to the letter between February and October 1917.

#### 3. Lenin's April Theses (1917), War Communism, and the War Against Reality

When **Vladimir Lenin** arrived at Petrograd's Finland Station in April 1917 and issued his **April Theses**, followed by the Bolshevik coup in October, he systematically inverted all Five Principles of Reality:

* **Against Principle 1:** Objective truth and scientific impartiality were abolished in favor of *partiynost* ("Party-mindedness").
* **Against Principle 3 and Principle 4:** Under **War Communism (1918–1921)** and later Stalin's **Forced Collectivization (1929–1933)**, the Bolsheviks waged literal war against the exact class of competent, industrious family farmers (*kulaks*) whom Stolypin had empowered. Notice the horrific comedy of the word ***kulak***: in practice, any peasant who kept his barn roof repaired, owned two cows or a metal plow, and harvested more grain per acre than his lazy neighbor was branded a "class enemy," stripped of his home and tools, and shot or deported in freezing boxcars to the Arctic wilderness!
* **Against Principle 5:** Independent courts and symmetrical statutes were replaced by the Cheka's **"Revolutionary Conscience"**—explicitly instructing tribunals not to look for evidence of what crime a defendant had committed, but first to ask **what class he belonged to, what his parents were, and what his education was.**
* **The Consequence in Reality:** Once the competent farmers were shot or deported and the remaining peasants were herded back into state collectives where they owned nothing they grew, agricultural production collapsed and **more than ten million human beings starved to death** across the Volga, Ukraine (the *Holodomor*), and Kazakhstan in one of the richest black-earth grain baskets on the planet. Reality does not care about revolutionary slogans; kill the builders and abolish ownership, and the granary goes empty.

### 3. Iran (1978–1983) and the Mechanics of Modern Red-Green Tactical Alliances

Turn now to a third historical laboratory—**Iran between 1978 and 1983**—because it illuminates one of the most puzzling spectacles in modern politics: **How can two movements whose positive worldviews flatly contradict each other work together in lockstep to tear down a civilization?**

#### 1. The Shah's Warning: "The Unholy Alliance of Red and Black"

In the 1970s, Iran under **Mohammad Reza Shah Pahlavi** was rapidly industrializing, building modern universities, expanding secular courts, and educating women. The regime had real flaws and political opposition—**wait and look at the two forces that combined in 1978 to bring down the Iranian state**:

1. **The "Red" Faction (Marxist-Leninists and Secular Left-Wing Intellectuals):** The Communist **Tudeh Party**, the Marxist-Leninist *Fedaiyan-e Khalq*, the Islamic-Marxist *Mujahedin-e Khalq* (MEK), and Western-educated progressive university students who dreamed of a socialist workers' republic, gender egalitarianism, and the overthrow of Western capitalism.
2. **The "Black/Green" Faction (Khomeini's Theocratic Islamists):** **Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini** and his militant clerical network, whose explicit goal was to abolish secular law, impose strict *sharia* gender segregation and veiling, execute apostates and "corrupters on earth," and establish absolute rule by Islamic jurists (*Velayat-e Faqih*).

On paper, a Marxist feminist student in Tehran and a seventh-century theocratic mullah disagreed on almost every positive question of how human beings should live. **Why, then, did they march arm-in-arm in the streets of Tehran throughout 1978?**

Because the Shah called them by their true name: ***"the unholy alliance of Red and Black."***

To understand how such an alliance works—and why it always ends in blood—picture **Two Demolition Crews Pulling Down One Stone Wall**:

> **Two Crews Pulling Down One Wall:** Around a prosperous city stands a high stone constitutional wall that protects the homes, shops, and courts inside. Outside that wall stand two rival gangs: Crew A wants to burn the city down and build a Communist barracks on the ashes; Crew B wants to burn the city down and build a seventh-century theocratic fortress on the ashes.
>
> Neither Crew A nor Crew B has enough muscle to batter down the stone wall by itself. So on Monday morning, the two crews tie their ropes to the same timber ram and pull together with all their might, chanting the same negative slogan: *"Down with the Wall!"*
>
> Inside the city, naive observers look out the window and say: *"How absurd to claim Crew A and Crew B are working together! Crew A is atheist and progressive; Crew B is fundamentalist and patriarchal! They couldn't possibly be allies!"*
>
> Those naive observers confuse **positive blueprint** with **negative demolition**. While the wall is still standing, two incompatible wrecking crews have a **100 percent shared interest in pulling down the host wall that restrains them both!**

And what happened in Iran on the morning *after* the wall came down in February 1979?

The Tudeh Communist leaders and secular progressive professors smiled and expected to share cabinet ministries in a new revolutionary utopia. Instead, between **1979 and 1983**, Khomeini's clerics—who controlled the mosques, the street militias (*Komitehs*), and the new Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps—systematically closed every independent newspaper, purged the universities in the "Islamic Cultural Revolution," forced every woman back under the mandatory veil on pain of flogging, outlawed the Tudeh Party and leftist organizations, and **rounded up, tortured, and executed thousands of their former Marxist and student allies inside Evin Prison.**

When two anti-procedural wolves team up to kill the shepherd, they do not become vegetarians after the shepherd is dead; **the stronger, more ruthless wolf immediately eats the weaker wolf for breakfast.**

#### 2. The Five Mechanics of Modern Red-Green Coordination in the West

Today, in the streets, universities, city councils, and NGOs of Western constitutional democracies, we watch the exact same **Red-Green Tactical Alliance** (radical progressive/Marxist activists marching arm-in-arm with political Islamist organizations):

* Why do radical progressive activists who denounce traditional Christian bakers over pronouns simultaneously march alongside Islamist groups whose classical legal doctrine prescribes severe punishment for homosexuality and legal subordination for women and religious minorities?
* Because **both share the immediate tactical goal of subverting and paralyzing the host Western constitutional order!**

Watch the **five operational mechanics** by which this Red-Green alliance operates inside an open society:

1. **The Shield and the Sword:** The Islamist movement supplies street intimidation, demographic mobilization, and ruthless sectarian solidarity (**the Sword**), while the progressive academic/media/legal apparatus supplies ideological cover (**the Shield**)—immediately branding any citizen, police officer, or magistrate who tries to enforce symmetrical law against Islamist intimidation as a *"racist"* or *"Islamophobe."*
2. **Dual-Code Speech:** Speaking one language in English to progressive television cameras (using the borrowed vocabulary of "civil rights," "inclusion," and "minority protection" to demand institutional concessions) while speaking a completely different language internally in sectarian halls (the language of conquest, demographic replacement, and religious supremacy).
3. **Humiliation Politics in Public Space:** Deliberately blocking major highways, bridges, train stations, and civic monuments—not to persuade fellow citizens, but to **visibly demonstrate that the host police and courts are too timid to enforce the law against them**, thereby demoralizing law-abiding citizens and signaling who owns the street.
4. **Speech Sacralization and Blasphemy Shields:** Conditioning a free society to accept that while its own heritage, constitution, and majority religion may be mocked and torn down daily on taxpayer salaries, criticizing the allied faction's dogma is "hate speech" or "incitement" that must be censored by the state or punished by street violence.
5. **Institutional Entryism:** Using the host society's own "diversity and inclusion" mandates to place factional activists inside school boards, police oversight panels, human-rights commissions, and prosecutor offices, where they enforce **procedural asymmetry** (Marcuse's *Repressive Tolerance* in action).

**How does a healthy civilization defeat this coordination?** Not by abandoning its own principles or adopting right-wing authoritarianism, **but by enforcing Principle 5 (Scoped Procedural Symmetry) with granite backbone**:

* Zero tolerance for highway blockades, street intimidation, or political violence, regardless of which flag the rioter is waving.
* Zero blasphemy shields or speech codes: one symmetrical rule of free speech and public order for every citizen.
* Zero public funding or legal jurisdiction for any organization that operates as an arm of a foreign state or seeks to replace constitutional symmetry with sectarian law.
