## Chapter 9: Principle 5: Justice Is Scoped Procedural Symmetry.

Examine the institutional problem created by placing Principle 4 next to Principle 2.

Under **Principle 2**, human beings are self-interested, limited, and particular. Under **Principle 4**, every good father, mother, and brother rightly prioritizes his own household and kin above strangers. That tiered loyalty is the primary motive force that builds homes, clears land, and raises children.

Now ask a direct question of institutional design: **What happens when ten thousand self-anchored, family-loyal households must share the same town, the same river, the same highway, the same marketplace, and the same army?**

Observe what happens if Principle 4 (direct loyalty to one's own kin and inner circle) is permitted to govern the municipal courtroom or the licensing board:

* Suppose your neighbor's brother steals your work truck at midnight. You bring the thief into the municipal court the next morning, and you discover that the judge on the bench is the thief's older brother. If the judge applies Principle 4 from the bench (*"Kinship comes first; I must protect my brother"*), he dismisses the case and allows his brother to keep your truck. Tomorrow morning, you and your cousins will not resort to the court; you will arm yourselves, march to the judge's house, and recover the truck by force. Within six months, the town resembles a failed state: a collection of armed family compounds conducting blood feuds across the street.
* Or suppose an aviation examiner at the flight licensing board is testing candidates for commercial airline captaincies. Five strangers pass the rigorous cockpit emergency simulation, while the examiner's own nephew panics and fails the simulation three times in succession. If the examiner applies inner-tier family favoritism at the licensing desk and certifies his incompetent nephew regardless, he sends three hundred passengers, including his own neighbors' children, to their deaths against a mountain on the first icy night.

Even in ancient China, where filial devotion to family was revered as the highest personal virtue, thinkers encountered this exact tension. In the ***Analects* (13.18)**, the Duke of She remarked to **Confucius** that in his district, a man was considered so "upright" that when his father stole a sheep, the son testified against his own father. Confucius replied that in his home district, uprightness meant the opposite: *"The father conceals the misconduct of the son, and the son conceals the misconduct of the father."*

Consider Confucius's sheep carefully. It is natural to sympathize with a son's distress when his father commits an offense; that is why constitutional protections in modern law excuse spouses from being compelled to testify against each other while still prosecuting the crime through independent evidence. **Yet what happens if the entire town adopts "every clan conceals its own sheep-stealers" as its rule of public justice?** Then the shepherd whose sheep was stolen has no judicial remedy against any thief who belongs to a large clan. To feed his own children, the shepherd must form a larger faction and steal two sheep in return. Clan concealment does not preserve families; it destroys the public peace that keeps every family's livestock secure.

How, then, can thousands of family-loyal households cooperate, trade, form corporations, staff armies, and adjudicate disputes within a single society without degenerating into nepotism, corruption, and blood feuds?

Only through **Principle 5**:

> **5. Justice is scoped procedural symmetry.**

Three terms follow the verb: **scoped**, **procedural**, **symmetry**. Every word in this definition is essential to the structure of justice. Remove any one of them, and what people call "justice" immediately degenerates into anarchy, tyranny, or legalized plunder. Let us examine each term in turn.

### 1. "Scoped": Boundaries, Roles, Legitimate Power, and Borders

The first word of justice is **scoped**.

Why must justice be *scoped*? Because a rule has meaning only when one knows **where the rule applies, to whom it applies, and what position each person occupies inside that boundary.** An unscoped rule, a rule that treats every human being on earth as standing in the exact same relationship to every house, every contract, every classroom, and every courtroom, is an impossibility in reality.

Examine the three distinct layers of **scope**:

#### Layer A: Jurisdictional Boundaries (Where the Rule Applies)

Every legitimate system of rules operates within a defined perimeter:

* **The Household Scope:** Inside my house, my wife and I establish the dinner hour, the budget, and the bedtime. My neighbor down the street may be an honorable man, but he has no jurisdiction to enter my kitchen and vote on what my children eat for supper, and I have no jurisdiction to vote on his.
* **The Contractual or Associational Scope:** If five master carpenters form a partnership and sign a contract agreeing to divide workshop profits evenly according to hours recorded in the shop ledger, that rule binds the five partners who signed the contract. A passerby on the sidewalk cannot walk into the shop on Friday afternoon and demand one-sixth of the proceeds in the name of "equality," because he is outside the scope of the partnership.
* **The Municipal and National Scope:** The traffic laws, tax statutes, and criminal courts of Ohio apply inside the borders of Ohio; they do not apply in Tokyo or Paris.

#### Layer B: Positional Scope (Roles Inside the Boundary)

Even inside the *same* jurisdiction or institution, people occupy **distinct functional positions** that carry distinct rights and duties:

* Inside a university engineering course, the **professor** and the **students** are both inside the scope of the classroom. Does justice mean that the students grade the professor's examination while the professor takes the test? Of course not. Procedural symmetry inside that classroom means that **every student sitting in the position of "student" is graded by the exact same standard**, while the professor occupying the position of "examiner" is bound by his duty to apply that standard honestly.
* Inside a courtroom, the **judge**, the **jurors**, the **prosecutor**, and the **defendant** occupy distinct positions. Symmetry means that *every defendant* standing before the bar receives the same rules of evidence and burden of proof, not that the defendant is permitted to strike the gavel and sentence the judge.
* Inside a nation, a **citizen** and a **foreign visitor** (or an uninvited border-crosser) do not occupy the same legal position. When a foreign tourist visits your country lawfully, justice requires that he be protected from robbery and tried fairly if accused of a crime (the scope of basic criminal law); it does *not* mean that a tourist who arrived at the airport twenty minutes ago is entitled to vote in your national election, draw from your contributory pension fund, or sit on your military intelligence committee (the scope of citizenship).

#### Layer C: How Scope Defines the Nature of a Polity

One can determine the character of any civilization immediately by asking how it defines the **scope of full legal symmetry** in its public courts:

* **Classical Islamic Law (*Fiqh*):** In the classical caliphates and the Ottoman Empire, the scope of legal symmetry was explicitly **religious**. Full legal symmetry applied within the scope of the *ummah* (free male Muslim believers). Conquered Christian, Jewish, and Zoroastrian communities (*dhimmis*) were placed in a separate, explicitly subordinate legal scope under the pact conditions derived from **Qur'an 9:29** and the **Pact of Umar**: they were permitted to live and practice their faith in private only so long as they paid the special tribute head-tax (***jizya***) *"with willing submission, and feel themselves subdued,"* were barred from holding authority over Muslims, could not build new churches above Muslim rooflines, and did not have equal evidentiary standing when testifying against a Muslim in an Islamic court. Within its own declared religious scope, classical *fiqh* was internally consistent and rule-bound. Notice that it was not random disorder; it possessed courts, judges (*qadis*), and legal treatises, **but its scope permanently divided inhabitants of the same land into superior and subordinate legal tiers by religion.**
* **Constitutional Civic Citizenship:** By contrast, Western constitutional law after the British and American constitutional settlements defined the public scope of the courtroom by **common citizenship under a single law**. Inside the courtroom of a constitutional republic, whether the plaintiff and defendant are Protestant, Catholic, Jewish, or atheist, wealthy merchant or poor laborer, they occupy the single position of **citizen before the law**, and the impartial court applies the exact same rules of contract, property, and evidence to both.

#### Deriving Legitimate Power: The Parent-Child Model and the Vacancy of Authority

Before we leave the word *scoped*, we must dispel a widespread modern misconception regarding **power and authority**.

In modern academic and activist circles, one frequently hears the claim that *all* power and hierarchy are inherently oppressive, that whenever one person holds authority over another, an injustice is occurring.

What happens when conscientious people accept that claim and refuse to exercise authority?

In every human association larger than a few individuals, governing decisions must be enforced: someone will determine whether thieves are prosecuted or released, whether children are taught sound instruction or partisan dogma, and whether public revenue maintains civic infrastructure or enriches favored factions.

If honest, self-governed citizens refuse to exercise magistracy and institutional authority out of a reluctance to judge others, sovereign power does not cease to exist. It remains vacant only until the most unscrupulous faction, bureaucrat, or criminal seizes that jurisdiction and exercises it over the households of the conscientious. Refusing to exercise legitimate authority within one's proper scope is not humility; it is **a dereliction of duty that surrenders governance to the least principled members of society.**

Where, then, do we find the natural model of **legitimate power**? Examine the foundational human scope: **the loving father and mother raising a young child.**

* Does a parent hold real, asymmetric authority over a four-year-old child? Yes: the parent commands the child not to run into highway traffic, requires the child to eat nourishing food and go to bed at eight o'clock, and disciplines the child when he strikes his sister.
* Why is the parent's authority legitimate, whereas a kidnapper's or tyrant's power is wrongful? Because the parent's authority is **strictly scoped to his role and exercised for the purpose of keeping, building, and maturing the child into a capable, independent adult** (Principles 3 and 4).
* By contrast, a tyrant exercises power to keep his subjects permanently weak, dependent, and exploited for his own gratification.

That is the universal test of legitimate authority in every scope, from a household to a workshop to an aircraft cockpit to a nation's magistracy: **Power is legitimate when it is bounded by a real scope of responsibility and exercised to keep and build the lives entrusted to that scope.**

#### Borders, the Winter Storehouse, and Naturalization as Civic Adoption

Apply this law of scope directly to the **national border**.

A house without walls and a roof is not a "welcoming house"; it is merely an open patch of ground exposed to the wind and rain. In the exact same way, **a country without enforced borders is not a compassionate polity; it is a dissolved jurisdiction** where no law, no civic trust, and no shared treasury can survive.

To see why, consider a mountain farming community preparing for winter:

> **The Winter Storehouse:** From May through October, forty farming families in a cold mountain valley work twelve hours a day plowing rocky soil, sowing grain, weeding gardens, splitting ten cords of firewood per house, and salting meat into barrels inside the village storehouse. Every pound of flour and every split log in that storehouse is **accumulated summer labor** stored against the six-month freeze from November to April.
>
> In January, when the snow is four feet deep, suppose a theorist stands at the storehouse door and declares: *"Boundaries are arbitrary lines. Everyone on the mountain has an equal human right to the grain and firewood inside this building, regardless of whether they belonged to this village, helped plant in May, or intend to help plow next spring."*
>
> If the village unbolts the storehouse doors to five hundred strangers who never planted a furrow and acknowledge no obligation to the village laws, what happens? The grain and firewood are exhausted by the second week of February, the seed corn for next spring is consumed, and **both the villagers and the newcomers freeze and starve together in March.**

Furthermore, in the modern world of sovereign states, a nation that fails to scope its borders and domestic institutions becomes vulnerable to **Asymmetric Mobilization**:

* An open constitutional society rightly separates private business, charity, education, and state warfare inside its domestic scope: when a domestic baker buys land or opens a school, he is simply a citizen operating a business or a school.
* An authoritarian rival state (such as the Chinese Communist Party under its explicit "Military-Civil Fusion" and "United Front" doctrines, or the Russian or Iranian security states) **recognizes no such separation**. To a total-mobilization regime, its corporations, sovereign wealth funds, diaspora cultural centers, student associations, and state-backed land purchasers operating inside your country are all **coordinated instruments of state strategic warfare**.
* If an open society naively extends domestic procedural symmetry to the agents and front-companies of a hostile foreign state (allowing them to purchase farmland adjacent to airbases, control port cranes and telecommunications grids, fund university departments, or exploit domestic court discovery), that open society is inviting destruction through a **category error**. It is treating Tier 4 and Tier 5 foreign state operations as if they were domestic citizens. Justice is *scoped*: foreign states and their directed organs stand outside the domestic citizen scope and must be met with sovereign boundary defenses.

How, then, should a sound nation admit newcomers into its citizen scope? Through the classical Roman and constitutional model of **Naturalization as Civic Adoption**:

* When a family adopts a child, it does not open the front door to fifty strangers at once so that the household collapses. It adopts **selectively**, in numbers the family can genuinely feed, care for, and acculturate.
* And what did Roman civic adoption require of an adopted son? It required ***pietas***: wholehearted filial allegiance to the adopting household, its laws, its ancestors, and its future. An immigrant who seeks naturalization because he respects the host nation's constitutional order, learns its language, obeys its laws, works productively, and binds his children's future to his new country becomes a genuine fellow citizen in the polity. Conversely, someone who treats a nation's passport as a mere instrument of financial convenience while retaining primary political loyalty to a foreign faction, or importing the very tribal and sectarian feuds that ruined his former homeland, violates the foundational covenant of civic adoption.

### 2. "Procedural": Known Rules, Unequal Outcomes, Judgment, and Two-Part Forgiveness

Turn now to the second word of Principle 5: **Justice is scoped *procedural* symmetry.**

Why **procedural**? What is a procedure?

A **procedure** is a clear, repeatable method of action and adjudication whose rules are **stated in advance, known to all participants before the contest or dispute begins, and applied step-by-step to the evidence of what a person actually did.**

#### Rule of Law Versus Rule by Man (and Why Impartial Rules Cannot Depend on Identity)

Consider entering an athletic contest where no rulebook is written before play begins. Instead, the referee announces: *"Play hard; whenever I blow my whistle, I will look at the scoreboard, consider how I feel about each team's historical background, and award points to whichever team I believe deserves to feel better at that moment."*

Within five minutes, the contest disintegrates. Why? Because when rules are invented after the action or altered according to which party is before the tribunal, **skill, preparation, and honest effort become pointless, and lobbying or intimidating the referee becomes the dominant strategy.**

In political and legal history, this is the fundamental dividing line between the **Rule of Law** (procedural justice) and **Rule by Man** (arbitrary decree by monarchs, commissars, mobs, or administrative bureaucrats):

* When **Solon** reformed the laws of **Athens** in 594 BCE, he had the statutes inscribed onto rotating wooden and stone tablets (*axones* and *kyrbeis*) erected in the public marketplace where every citizen could read them in advance, binding magistrate and poor farmer to the exact same procedure. Even the ancient Near Eastern proverb of **"the law of the Medes and Persians, which alters not"** captured an essential discovery of early statecraft: once a decree was entered into the public law, not even the monarch could arbitrarily rewrite it to favor a courtier today.
* Contrast Solon's procedural Athens with **Lycurgus's Sparta**, which collectivists from Rousseau to modern authoritarians have praised for its supposed "equality" and martial discipline. Examine the actual mechanics of Sparta's outcome-equality:

    1. First, the "equality" of the Spartiate warrior caste (*Homoioi*) rested entirely on the enslavement of a massive subject population, the **Helots**, who outnumbered Spartans roughly seven to one and worked the land under threat of death. Every year, the Spartan magistrates formally declared war on their own helot laborers so that young Spartans in the secret police (***Krypteia***) could patrol the countryside at night and kill any helot who appeared too strong, intelligent, or industrious without incurring religious blood-guilt.
    2. Second, inside the Spartan state upbringing (*agoge*), how were Spartan boys trained? They were deliberately underfed and **commanded to steal food from farms and storehouses**, and if a boy was caught stealing, he was flogged severely, **not because stealing was wrong, but because he had been clumsy enough to be caught.** Consider what that did to the moral formation of a society: it taught from childhood that property and truth have no intrinsic status, and that only stealth, force, and state utility matter. Unsurprisingly, historical accounts repeatedly noted that whenever Spartan commanders went abroad away from domestic surveillance, they were notoriously susceptible to bribery; and when Thebes finally defeated the Spartan phalanx at the Battle of Leuctra in 371 BCE and liberated the Messenian helots, Sparta shrank into an insignificant village that left behind not a single work of science, philosophy, architecture, or engineering.

#### Why Fair Procedure Applied to Particular Humans Produces Unequal Outcomes

Connect Principle 5 directly back to **Principle 2** (*Humans are self-interested, limited, and particular*).

Because human beings are **particular**, differing in innate talent, physical stamina, conscientiousness, family preparation, and daily effort, what happens whenever one applies an **identical, scrupulously fair procedure** to a hundred particular individuals?

**One obtains unequal outcomes in every instance.**

Observe this in a classroom or licensing hall:

* A civil engineering professor administers the exact same **Structural Mechanics Final Examination** to a hundred students. Every student had access to the same lectures, the same textbook, and the same two hours in a quiet room. The examinations are graded anonymously by student identification number so the professor cannot see any student's name, race, sex, or family background.
* Student A spent twelve hours a week all semester solving practice problems and has a strong aptitude for spatial mathematics; he scores **98 out of 100**.
* Student B spent two hours a week studying and struggles with calculus; he scores **54 out of 100**.
* **Was the examination unjust because Student A and Student B received unequal scores?** No. The unequal outcome is the **truthful measurement** of different preparation and competence under an identical procedure.
* What happens if the university dean intervenes and declares, *"Unequal scores are proof of systemic injustice; we must eliminate advanced classes, discard the examination, and grant both Student A and Student B the exact same Structural Engineer license"*? Two failures occur simultaneously: first, **the institution confiscates the reward of Student A's honest labor** and teaches the next class of students that diligent study is pointless; second, **it falsifies reality** (violating Principle 1) and certifies an incompetent engineer whose bridge will collapse under innocent travelers.

#### The Necessity of Judgment: Why "Non-Judgmentalism" Is Self-Destructive

Notice that a procedure is useless unless human beings have the courage to render a **verdict** at the end of the procedure: *True or false. Pass or fail. Honest merchant or fraudster. Guilty or not guilty.*

In a decadent culture, one constantly hears the slogan *"Who am I to judge? We must never judge anyone's choices or way of life."*

Test that slogan against reality:

* Can a carpenter build a house that stands in a gale if he refuses to **judge** between seasoned white oak and decayed, dry-rotted pine?
* Can a young woman build a sound marriage and raise safe children if she refuses to **judge** between a suitor who is sober, truthful, and industrious and a suitor who is a violent, habitual liar?
* Can a town remain safe if juries refuse to **judge** between the shopkeeper who worked sixty hours a week and the burglar who broke his window with a brick?

**Discriminating judgment, the application of truthful standards to reality and action upon the verdict, is the protective defense of human life.** Just as a body whose immune system ceases to distinguish between healthy host cells and invading bacteria perishes of infection, a society that refuses to distinguish between builder and offender perishes of disorder. What Principle 5 forbids is *arbitrary prejudice* (pre-judging a person by faction or bribe before examining the evidence); what Principle 5 commands is **accurate procedural judgment** based on conduct and reality.

#### Mercy Versus Permissiveness, and the Structure of Two-Part Forgiveness

At this point, a thoughtful reader asks: *"If justice is strict procedural symmetry and truthful judgment, where do **mercy** and **forgiveness** belong? Are we required to be devoid of mercy?"*

On the contrary: **only a society with clear procedural justice can practice genuine mercy and genuine forgiveness**, because without a standard of judgment, what people call "mercy" is actually destructive **permissiveness**.

Examine closely the difference between **Mercy** and **Permissiveness**:

* **Mercy** begins by stating the complete truth about the law and the wrong: *"The law against theft is sound and remains in force. You stole fifty dollars from this shopkeeper, and under the statute you owe full restitution plus thirty days in jail. Because this is your first offense, you have confessed truthfully, you have repaid the fifty dollars from your own wages, and your father has vouched to supervise your work this summer, the court exercises **mercy** to suspend the jail sentence on condition of lawful conduct."* Notice that genuine mercy **upholds the standard, requires ownership and restitution, protects the innocent victim, and is scoped to a repentant offender.**
* **Permissiveness (Counterfeit Mercy)**, by contrast, refuses to enforce the law or identify the wrong as a wrong. When a prosecutor or judge observes a habitual thief steal from a neighborhood grocery store for the twentieth time and releases him onto the street an hour later with no restitution and no penalty while lecturing the shopkeeper about "compassion for the marginalized," that judge has not shown mercy. **Permissiveness toward the unrepentant offender is cruelty toward every honest grocer, mother, and child on that street.**

The exact same structural distinction resolves one of the most difficult personal questions in human life: **When someone has betrayed, defrauded, or harmed you, what does it mean to forgive him?**

Confusing two separate scopes of forgiveness ruins many lives, either trapping victims in lifelong bitterness, or inducing victims through false guilt to allow an unrepentant offender back into their household to harm them again. Separate the two distinct operations of **Two-Part Forgiveness**:

1. **Part 1: Personal Release of Bitterness (Unilateral: Within One's Own Mind, Tier 1).**

    - This first part of forgiveness is **unilateral**: it does not require the offender's permission, apology, or presence.
    - What is it? It is the deliberate decision within one's own mind to commit the offender to lawful justice and reality, and to **dismiss rancor, obsessive hatred, and vengeful brooding from your own mind** so that the person who harmed you does not continue to occupy your thoughts for the next thirty years and impair your marriage, your work, and your rest.
    - One can and should perform Part 1 even if the offender is dead, in prison, or completely unrepentant, because Part 1 keeps *one's own* mind clear and functional.

2. **Part 2: Social Reconciliation and Restored Trust (Bilateral and Procedural: Between Two Persons in Tiers 2–4).**

    - The second part (restoring the relationship, admitting the person back into your house, lending him your vehicle again, or appointing him treasurer of the company) is **never unilateral**. It is **strictly procedural and conditional upon the offender's real-world conduct**.
    - What is the procedure for restored trust?

        1. **Truth (Virtue 1):** The offender must cease lying, abandon all excuses, and admit plainly what he did.
        2. **Ownership and Restitution (Virtue 2):** He must repay what he stole or repair what he damaged to the fullest extent possible.
        3. **Tested Change Over Time:** Trust is lost rapidly and restored gradually. A brother who stole from your cash drawer does not receive the safe combination the afternoon he says "I am sorry"; he earns back limited scopes of trust over months and years of verified honesty.

    - When an offender genuinely completes those steps, a prudent community provides a defined pathway back into productive life, because if a society offers no path of return for a repentant person who has paid his debt, it forces every offender into permanent outlawry.
    - Conversely, if an offender refuses to admit the truth, blames you for his offense, and continues the behavior, **you can complete Part 1 (hold no personal bitterness) while keeping your door barred against him under Part 2.** Releasing bitterness is charity; entrusting your property to an unrepentant thief is folly.

### 3. "Symmetry": The Blindfolded Scale, Boundary Conditions, and the Two Counterfeits

Now examine the third word of Principle 5: **Justice is scoped procedural *symmetry*.**

What does **symmetry** mean in geometry and engineering? It means that if you interchange the labels of two parties who occupy the **same position inside the same scope**, the procedure does not change at all.

* If Rich Citizen A breaches a written contract with Poor Citizen B, the court applies Procedure $X$.
* If Poor Citizen B breaches the exact same written contract with Rich Citizen A, the court applies the **exact same Procedure $X$**.
* That is why the classical statue of Lady Justice wears a **blindfold**: not because she is blind to the facts and evidence of the case, but because she refuses to consider the wealth, political party, skin color, or family connections of the litigants before weighing the evidence on her scale.

#### Two Crucial Boundary Conditions of Symmetry

Because symmetry operates inside a procedure, two boundary conditions govern how it handles actors who attempt to subvert the procedure itself:

1. **Citizens Inside the Law vs. Armed Enemies Outside the Law:**

    Domestic criminal procedure (search warrants, police cautions, bail hearings, proof beyond a reasonable doubt before twelve jurors) is the proper symmetrical procedure **within the scope of domestic civic peace**. When an armed foreign army invades your territory, or a terrorist cell is actively firing rockets at your cities, they are operating in the scope of **warfare**, not domestic tort law. Treating an active military enemy on the battlefield as if he were a domestic misdemeanor defendant is a fatal confusion of scope.

2. **Honest Error vs. Deliberate Fraud (Corrupting the Procedure):**

    Inside a peaceful community, two very different kinds of harm occur:

    - **Honest Error / Negligence:** A driver glances at his mirror at the wrong second and dents your bumper, or a bookkeeper accidentally transposes two digits on an invoice and immediately corrects it when pointed out. Here, ordinary symmetrical civil restitution (paying to repair the bumper or correcting the invoice) restores the balance.
    - **Deliberate Fraud (Attacking the Procedure Itself):** A merchant secretly hollows out his brass scale weights and fills them with cork; a litigant forges a property deed or bribes a juror; an academic fabricates laboratory data; a voter casts ten fraudulent ballots.
    - Why must deliberate **fraud** be punished far more severely than an honest mistake of the same monetary amount? Because **an honest error occurs *within* the procedure, whereas fraud is an attack upon the tribunal, the rules, and the standard of measurement itself.** In a high-trust society, millions of daily transactions proceed rapidly and at low cost because people can rely on weights, deeds, certificates, and oaths without hiring three armed guards and a forensic chemist for every loaf of bread. A fraudster exploits that shared trust and forces the entire society to slow down and bear a permanent security cost. Therefore, procedural symmetry requires that anyone caught deliberately falsifying the procedure face severe, exemplary penalties, including treble damages, forfeiture of license, and imprisonment.

#### Refuting the Two Counterfeits of Symmetry

Whenever a civilization forgets that symmetry belongs to **procedure**, it falls into one of two destructive counterfeits: **Outcome Symmetry ("Equity")** or **Value Symmetry (Retaliatory Feuding)**. Let us examine both:

#### Counterfeit 1: Outcome Symmetry ("Equity") and the Equalizer Class

Modern ideological activists explicitly draw a contrast between *Equality* (which they define as equal procedural rules at the starting line) and ***Equity*** (which they define as **Outcome Symmetry**: guaranteeing that every person or group arrives at the exact same result regardless of performance).

Ask three simple engineering questions about Outcome Symmetry:

1. **How can one force unequal inputs to produce equal outputs?** Only by **destroying procedural symmetry**, that is, by biasing the rules in reverse. If Runner A runs the hundred-yard dash in ten seconds and Runner B runs it in fifteen seconds, the only way to make them cross the finish line at the exact same instant is to attach fifty pounds of lead to Runner A's ankles or move Runner B's starting blocks thirty-five yards down the track. **Outcome Symmetry requires Procedural Asymmetry.** One cannot have both.
2. **Where does Outcome Symmetry naturally converge?** Recall Principle 3: building excellence requires exceptional skill and labor, whereas failure and death require no effort at all. One cannot enact a statute that confers upon every student on earth the mathematical genius of Isaac Newton or the surgical skill of the world's finest neurosurgeon. **One can never equalize human beings upward to the highest level of performance; one can only equalize them downward to the lowest common denominator.** Place a pilot who passed the flight examination and a pilot who failed the flight examination into an ice storm under a mandate of "equal outcomes": one cannot decree that both aircraft land safely; **one can guarantee equal outcomes only by causing the competent pilot's aircraft to crash alongside the incompetent one.**
3. **Who holds the scale in an "Equity" regime?** This is the inevitable political consequence of every outcome-equalizing movement in history: if the state promises to continually adjust the rules, confiscate property, and reassign positions until every statistical group has identical outcomes, **someone must be granted permanent, unchecked discretionary power to alter the rules every day.** That creates a permanent ruling class, an **Equalizer Class** of commissars, administrators, and ideological bureaucrats, who enjoy complete exemption from symmetry while exercising authority over everyone else. As George Orwell observed in *Animal Farm*, in every regime of outcome-equality, the equalizers who administer the rules always end up *"more equal than others."*

#### Counterfeit 2: Value Symmetry (Retaliatory Feuding and Status Pricing)

The older, pre-modern counterfeit of justice is **Value Symmetry**: the belief that justice consists in inflicting in return an equivalent "value" of blood or status injury, priced according to the rank or tribe of the parties.

Observe what happens in two historical forms of Value Symmetry:

* **The Unending Clan Feud Ledger:** In blood-feud societies (from medieval vendetta clans to highland feuds), Clan A accidentally kills a herdsman from Clan B. Clan B calculates that *their* dead man was worth two ordinary men of Clan A, so they ride over and kill two men of Clan A. Clan A, viewing its own sons through the natural partiality of inner-tier affection, regards the killing of two of its men as an unprovoked outrage worth four men of Clan B. **The feud ledger never closes**, because human beings are biased judges of their own injuries. Only when a third-party magistrate intervenes with a **scoped, uniform procedural law**, removing retaliatory force from private clan control, can the cycle of reprisal be terminated.
* **Status-Graded Retaliation (*Lex Talionis* and *Diyya* Schedules) vs. Uniform Statutory Punishment:** In many ancient codes, including parts of the Code of Hammurabi and classical tribal or sectarian blood-money (*diyya*) schedules, the penalty for killing or injuring a person explicitly varied by the victim's caste, sex, or religion: knocking out the tooth of a nobleman brought a severe penalty, while knocking out the tooth of a commoner or non-believer cost a few silver coins.
* Contrast that status-pricing with **uniform statutory punishment** under procedural symmetry, even when that punishment is severe and physical. Consider **Singapore's judicial caning** for violent robbery, vandalism, or extortion: the statute is written in advance in plain language; it applies identically to a wealthy foreign banker's son and a local dockworker convicted of the same offense; it is administered under strict medical supervision; and, unlike Western prisons that confine a young offender in a prison with hardened gang members for five years at heavy taxpayer expense while his family disintegrates, it delivers an immediate physical deterrent in ten minutes and returns the chastened offender to work and support his household the following week. Severity is not injustice; **arbitrary asymmetry is injustice.**

### 4. Human Rights Under the Five Principles: Scoped Constitutional Liberties Versus Modern "Human Rights" Ideology

Now that we understand all three words of Principle 5 (**scoped**, **procedural**, **symmetry**), we can resolve the confusion surrounding one of the most frequently misused phrases of the modern age: **"Human Rights."**

Why does the term "rights" sometimes denote the foundational liberties of a free, high-trust republic, and at other times operate as an ideological instrument that paralyzes law enforcement, exhausts public treasuries, and dissolves national borders?

Because two distinct concepts are concealed within the same term: **Classical Constitutional Rights** (which conform to Principle 5) and **Modern Universal "Human Rights" Ideology** (which systematically violates Principles 1, 3, 4, and 5). Let us distinguish them clearly:

#### 1. What Classical Constitutional Rights Actually Are: Scoped, Negative Procedural Symmetries

When the English Bill of Rights (1689), the American Declaration of Independence (1776), and the United States Constitution and Bill of Rights (1787–1791) spoke of unalienable rights, such as life, liberty, property, freedom of speech and conscience, the right to keep and bear arms, and trial by jury, examine what those rights actually were in institutional practice:

* **They were *Scoped* to a Specific Polity:** The American Bill of Rights was written as the supreme procedural law of the **United States of America**, enforced by American courts, defended by American soldiers, and maintained by the tax-paying, law-abiding citizens of that republic.
* **They were *Negative Procedural Boundaries* Against Arbitrary State Power:** Examine the phrasing of the First, Second, Fourth, and Fifth Amendments: *"Congress shall make no law..."* *"shall not be infringed..."* *"shall not be violated..."* *"nor be deprived of life, liberty, or property, without due process of law."*

    - Notice what a negative procedural right costs the public treasury in scarce material goods: **zero dollars.** For the magistrate to refrain from suppressing your speech, confiscating your rifle, or seizing your farm without a jury trial does not require a carpenter to build you a free house or a farmer to give you free wheat; it simply requires the state's officers to observe the same symmetrical boundary toward every citizen.

* **They were Joined to Civic Upkeep and Ownership (Virtue 2):** In the classical republic, the citizen whose property and speech were protected by the court was the exact same citizen who stood in the militia muster with his own rifle to defend the town, sat in the jury box to enforce the law against thieves, and supported his own household without depending upon his neighbors.

#### 2. The Five Structural Flaws of Modern "Human Rights" Ideology

### 5. International Law: Why Scope Runs Out at the Border and Military Might Is the Ultimate Arbiter

Closely tied to the illusion of unscoped "human rights" is a second misconception that confuses domestic politics with international reality: the belief in **"International Law."**

Whenever a foreign dictator invades a neighbor, seizes shipping lanes, or builds covert nuclear weapons, commentators on television solemnly announce: *"That action is a violation of International Law. The international community must refer this to the International Court,"* speaking as though the globe were a single municipality possessing a courthouse, a magistrate, and a police force ready to arrest the invader.

Let us state the operational truth about the international arena plainly:

> **Between sovereign nations, the scope of procedural symmetry runs out. There is no such thing as "international law" in the strong sense in which domestic law is law.**

Why? Trace the exact mechanics of **Principle 5 (*Justice is scoped procedural symmetry*)** and **Principle 4 (*Enemies are real*)**:

1. **Why Domestic Law Is Real Law (Inside the Polity Scope):**

    Why does a contract law, a property deed, or a murder statute work inside a functioning sovereign nation?

    Because inside that national perimeter, all citizens stand beneath **one shared sovereign authority** that possesses a **monopoly on legitimate physical enforcement**: a legislature that writes the statutes, courts that adjudicate disputes under a single procedure, and, standing directly behind the judge's gavel, **armed bailiffs, police officers, sheriffs, and soldiers who will compel entry, attach assets, and imprison the recalcitrant offender if he defies the court's judgment.** Without the sheriff and the prison, a domestic statute is merely an unenforceable recommendation.

2. **Why "International Law" Is Not Law (Outside the Polity Scope):**

    Now step outside the national border onto the international plane between sovereign states. Observe the conditions:

    - Is there a global constitution to which China, Russia, Iran, the United States, and Singapore have surrendered their sovereignty? **No.**
    - Is there a global legislature above nations? **No.**
    - Is there a global court with its own independent army and police force? **No.**
    - **Is there a global sheriff who can arrive in Beijing, Moscow, Tehran, or Washington in a police vehicle and arrest a sovereign head of state or seize an army corps?** **Never.**

3. **What Treaties and "International Law" Actually Are in Reality:**

    What people loosely call "international law" is actually a collection of **bilateral and multilateral treaties, customs, and coordination protocols** (governing matters such as maritime shipping lanes, radio frequencies, postal delivery, diplomatic pouches, and trade tariffs).

    And how long does any international treaty bind a sovereign state in physical reality?

    **A treaty binds only so long as one of two physical conditions holds true:**

    - **Condition A (Aligned Self-Interest, Principle 2):** Both sovereign parties continue to find it advantageous and profitable to keep the agreement (for example, both countries want their commercial airliners to land safely at each other's airports without colliding on the radio frequency); **or**
    - **Condition B (Credible Force, Principle 4):** A stronger military power stands ready and willing to make violating the treaty **catastrophically costly** in casualties, resources, and regime survival for the violator.

    Examine historical reality whenever both Condition A and Condition B disappear:

    - In **1928**, almost every major power on earth, including Germany, Japan, Italy, the Soviet Union, France, Britain, and the United States, solemnly signed the **Kellogg-Briand Pact**, officially outlawing war forever under "international law." Eleven years later, World War II killed seventy million people.
    - In **1994**, Ukraine signed the **Budapest Memorandum**, surrendering the third-largest nuclear weapons arsenal on earth in exchange for written security assurances from Russia, the United States, and Britain guaranteeing Ukraine's existing borders. When Ukraine surrendered the physical weapons (**Condition B**) and retained the paper treaty, what was the document worth when Russian armored forces crossed the border in 2014 and 2022? Nothing.

    **The only real international law in the ultimate sense is military might**, backed by the industrial base, demographic vitality, and national will that build and sustain military might. Even the historical eras that appeared most peaceful and "law-governed" across the oceans (such as the Roman Mediterranean under *Pax Romana*, nineteenth-century maritime trade under the British Royal Navy's *Pax Britannica*, or the post-1945 global shipping order under the United States Navy) appeared law-governed **only because a dominant military power was securing the sea lanes with naval force.** Mistake the security maintained by naval power for the permanent disappearance of piracy, and the moment the fleet is dismantled, piracy resumes.
