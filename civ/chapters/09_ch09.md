## Chapter 9: Principle 5 — Justice Is Scoped Procedural Symmetry.

Stop right here and notice the engineering problem we have just created by placing Principle 4 next to Principle 2.

Under **Principle 2**, human beings are self-interested, limited, and particular. Under **Principle 4**, every good father, mother, and brother rightly prioritizes his own household and kin above strangers. That tiered loyalty is the powerhouse that builds homes, clears creek banks, and raises children.

Now ask a simple engineering question: **What happens when ten thousand self-anchored, family-loyal households have to share the same town, the same river, the same highway, the same marketplace, and the same army?**

Watch what happens if Principle 4—raw loyalty to your own blood and your own inner circle—is allowed to run the town courtroom or the licensing office:

* Suppose your neighbor's brother steals your work truck at midnight. You haul the thief into the municipal court the next morning, and you discover that the judge on the bench is the thief's older brother. If the judge applies Principle 4 from the bench—*"Blood comes first! I must protect my brother!"*—he dismisses the case and lets his brother keep your truck. Tomorrow morning, you and your cousins will not bother going to court; you will load shotguns, march over to the judge's house, and take the truck back by blood feud. Within six months, the town is Somalia or Lebanon: a patchwork of armed family compounds shooting at one another across the street.
* Or suppose an aviation examiner at the flight licensing board is testing candidates for commercial airline captaincies. Five strangers pass the rigorous cockpit emergency simulation, and the examiner's own beloved nephew panics and crashes the simulator three times in a row. If the examiner applies inner-tier family favoritism to the licensing desk and stamps his incompetent nephew's certificate anyway, he puts three hundred passengers—including his own neighbors' children—into the side of a mountain on the first icy night.

Even in ancient China, where filial devotion to family was revered as the highest personal virtue, thinkers collided head-on with this tension. In the ***Analects* (13.18)**, the Duke of She boasted to **Confucius** that in his district, a man was considered so "upright" that when his father stole a sheep, the son testified against his own father. Confucius famously replied that in his home district, uprightness meant the opposite: *"The father conceals the misconduct of the son, and the son conceals the misconduct of the father."*

Pause and examine Confucius's sheep. It is easy to sympathize with a son's anguish when his father does wrong—that is why Fifth Amendment protections in modern law rightly excuse spouses from being forced to testify against each other while still prosecuting the crime through independent evidence. **But what if the whole town adopts "every clan covers up its own sheep-stealers" as its rule of public justice?** Then the shepherd whose sheep was stolen has no courtroom remedy against any thief who belongs to a large clan! To feed his own children, the shepherd has to form a bigger clan and steal two sheep back. Clan cover-up does not preserve families; it destroys the public peace that keeps every family's flock alive.

How, then, can thousands of fiercely family-loyal households cooperate, trade, build corporations, staff armies, and settle disputes inside a single society without degenerating into nepotism, corruption, and blood feuds?

Only through **Principle 5**:

> **5. Justice is scoped procedural symmetry.**

Four words after the verb: **scoped**, **procedural**, **symmetry**. Every single word in this definition is load-bearing steel. Remove any one of them, and what people call "justice" immediately turns into anarchy, tyranny, or legalized plunder. Let us walk slowly through each word in turn.

### 1. "Scoped": Boundaries, Roles, Legitimate Power, and Borders

The first word of justice is **scoped**.

Why must justice be *scoped*? Because a rule has meaning only when you know **where the rule applies, to whom it applies, and what position each person occupies inside that boundary.** An unscoped rule—a rule that pretends every human being on earth stands in the exact same relationship to every house, every contract, every classroom, and every courtroom—is an impossibility in reality.

Look at the three distinct layers of **scope**:

#### Layer A: Jurisdictional Boundaries (Where the Rule Applies)

Every legitimate system of rules operates within a defined perimeter:

* **The Household Scope:** Inside my house, my wife and I set the dinner hour, the budget, and the bedtime. My neighbor down the street is a wonderful man, but he has zero jurisdiction to walk into my kitchen and vote on what my children eat for supper—and I have zero jurisdiction to vote on his.
* **The Contractual or Associational Scope:** If five master carpenters form a partnership and sign a contract agreeing to split workshop profits evenly according to hours logged in the shop ledger, that rule binds the five partners who signed the contract. A passerby on the sidewalk cannot walk into the shop on Friday afternoon and demand one-sixth of the till in the name of "equality," because he is outside the scope of the partnership.
* **The Municipal and National Scope:** The traffic laws, tax statutes, and criminal courts of Ohio apply inside the borders of Ohio; they do not apply in Tokyo or Paris.

#### Layer B: Positional Scope (Roles Inside the Boundary)

Even inside the *same* jurisdiction or institution, people occupy **distinct functional positions** that carry distinct rights and duties:

* Inside a university engineering course, the **professor** and the **students** are both inside the scope of the classroom. Does justice mean that the students get to grade the professor's exam while the professor takes the test? Of course not! Procedural symmetry inside that classroom means that **every student sitting in the position of "student" is graded by the exact same rubric**, while the professor occupying the position of "examiner" is bound by his duty to apply that rubric honestly.
* Inside a courtroom, the **judge**, the **jurors**, the **prosecutor**, and the **defendant** occupy distinct positions. Symmetry means that *every defendant* standing in the dock receives the same rules of evidence and burden of proof—not that the defendant gets to bangs the gavel and sentence the judge.
* Inside a nation, a **citizen** and a **foreign visitor** (or an uninvited border-crosser) do not occupy the same legal position. When a foreign tourist visits your country lawfully, justice requires that he be protected from robbery and tried fairly if accused of a crime (the scope of basic criminal law); it does *not* mean that a tourist who landed at the airport twenty minutes ago gets to vote in your national election, draw from your contributory pension fund, or sit on your military intelligence committee (the scope of citizenship).

#### Layer C: How Scope Defines the Nature of a Polity

You can tell the character of any civilization immediately by asking how it defines the **scope of full legal symmetry** in its public courts:

* **Classical Islamic Law (*Fiqh*):** In the classical caliphates and the Ottoman Empire, the scope of legal symmetry was explicitly **religious**. Full legal symmetry applied within the scope of the *ummah* (free male Muslim believers). Conquered Christian, Jewish, and Zoroastrian communities (*dhimmis*) were placed in a separate, explicitly subordinate legal scope under the pact conditions derived from **Qur'an 9:29** and the **Pact of Umar**: they were permitted to live and practice their faith in private only so long as they paid the special tribute head-tax (***jizya***) *"with willing submission, and feel themselves subdued,"* were barred from holding authority over Muslims, could not build new churches above Muslim rooflines, and did not have equal evidentiary standing when testifying against a Muslim in an Islamic court. Within its own declared religious scope, classical *fiqh* was internally consistent and rule-bound—Wait and notice that! It was not random chaos; it had courts, judges (*qadis*), and treatises—**but its scope permanently divided inhabitants of the same land into superior and subordinate legal tiers by religion.**
* **Constitutional Civic Citizenship:** By contrast, Western constitutional law after the British and American constitutional settlements defined the public scope of the courtroom by **common citizenship under a single law**. Inside the courtroom of a constitutional republic, whether the plaintiff and defendant are Protestant, Catholic, Jewish, or atheist, rich merchant or poor laborer, they occupy the single position of **citizen before the law**, and the blindfolded scale applies the exact same rules of contract, property, and evidence to both.

#### Deriving Legitimate Power: The Parent-Child Model and the Bat in the Street

Before we leave the word *scoped*, we must clear away a modern neurosis about **power and authority**.

In modern academic and activist circles, you will often hear the claim that *all* power and hierarchy are inherently oppressive—that whenever one person holds authority over another, an injustice is occurring.

What happens when good, conscientious people believe that lie and refuse to exercise authority?

Picture a town square where a heavy wooden bat labeled **PUBLIC AUTHORITY** lies on the cobblestones. In every human gathering above three people, decisions must be enforced: someone is going to decide whether thieves go to jail or walk free, whether children learn arithmetic or ideology in the schoolhouse, and whether the city budget repairs the water main or lines the pockets of cronies.

If honest, self-controlled fathers, mothers, and builders look at the bat of power and say, *"Oh, wielding authority feels bossy and judgmental; we refuse to pick up that bat!"*—does the bat evaporate into thin air? **Never.** The bat stays right there on the cobblestones until the most ruthless ideologue, bureaucrat, or criminal in town walks over, picks it up with both hands, and uses it to rule the honest people's children. Refusing to wield legitimate authority in your proper scope is not humility; it is **dereliction of duty that hands the bat to the worst people in town.**

Where, then, do we see the natural blueprint of **legitimate power**? Look at the foundational human scope: **the loving father and mother raising a young child.**

* Does a parent hold real, asymmetric authority over a four-year-old child? Yes: the parent commands the child not to run into highway traffic, forces the child to eat vegetables and go to bed at eight o'clock, and disciplines the child when he hits his sister.
* Why is the parent's authority profoundly legitimate, whereas a kidnapper's or tyrant's power is evil? Because the parent's authority is **strictly scoped to his role and exercised for the purpose of keeping, building, and maturing the child into a capable, independent adult** (Principles 3 and 4).
* By contrast, a tyrant exercises power to keep his subjects permanently weak, dependent, and fleeced for his own gratification.

That is the universal test of legitimate authority in every scope—from a household to a workshop to a cockpit to a nation's magistracy: **Power is legitimate when it is bounded by a real scope of responsibility and wielded to keep and build the lives entrusted to that scope.**

#### Borders, the Winter Storehouse, and Naturalization as Civic Adoption

Apply this law of scope directly to the **national border**.

A house without walls and a roof is not a "welcoming house"; it is just an open patch of muddy ground exposed to the wind and rain. In the exact same way, **a country without enforced borders is not a compassionate polity; it is an dissolved jurisdiction** where no law, no civic trust, and no shared treasury can survive.

To see why, picture a mountain farming community preparing for winter:

> **The Winter Storehouse:** From May through October, forty farming families in a cold mountain valley work twelve hours a day plowing rocky soil, sowing grain, weeding gardens, splitting ten cords of firewood per house, and salting meat into barrels inside the village storehouse. Every pound of flour and every split log in that storehouse is **condensed summer labor** stored against the six-month freeze from November to April.
>
> In January, when the snow is four feet deep, suppose a philosopher stands at the storehouse door and declares: *"Boundaries are arbitrary lines! Everyone on the mountain has an equal human right to the grain and firewood inside this building, regardless of whether they belonged to this village, helped plant in May, or intend to help plow next spring!"*
>
> If the village unbolts the storehouse doors to five hundred passersby who never planted a furrow and acknowledge no obligation to the village laws, what happens? The grain and firewood are gone by the second week of February, the seed corn for next spring is eaten, and **both the villagers and the newcomers freeze and starve together in March.**

Furthermore, in the modern world of sovereign states, a nation that fails to scope its borders and domestic institutions falls prey to **Asymmetric Mobilization**:

* An open constitutional society rightly separates private business, charity, education, and state warfare inside its domestic scope: when a domestic baker buys land or opens a school, he is just a citizen running a business or a school.
* An authoritarian rival state (such as the Chinese Communist Party under its explicit "Military-Civil Fusion" and "United Front" doctrines, or the Russian or Iranian security states) **recognizes no such separation**. To a total-mobilization regime, its corporations, sovereign wealth funds, diaspora cultural centers, student associations, and state-backed land buyers operating inside your country are all **coordinated instruments of state strategic warfare**.
* If an open society naively extends domestic procedural symmetry to the agents and front-companies of a hostile foreign state—allowing them to buy up farmland next to airbases, control port cranes and telecommunications grids, fund university departments, or exploit domestic court discovery—that open society is committing suicide by **category error**. It is treating Tier 4/5 foreign state operations as if they were innocent domestic citizens. Justice is *scoped*: foreign states and their directed organs stand outside the domestic citizen scope and must be met with sovereign boundary defenses.

How, then, should a healthy nation admit newcomers into its citizen scope? Through the ancient Roman and constitutional model of **Naturalization as Civic Adoption**:

* When a family adopts a child, it does not fling open the front door to fifty strangers at once so that the house collapses. It adopts **selectively**, in numbers the family can genuinely feed, love, and acculturate.
* And what did Roman civic adoption require of an adopted son? It required ***pietas***—wholehearted filial allegiance to the adopting household, its laws, its ancestors, and its future. An immigrant who seeks naturalization because he admires the host nation's constitutional order, learns its language, obeys its laws, works productively, and binds his children's future to his new country becomes a genuine brother in the polity. Conversely, someone who treats a nation's passport as a convenient ATM card or hotel key while retaining primary political loyalty to a foreign faction—or importing the very tribal and religious feuds that ruined his former homeland—violates the foundational covenant of civic adoption.

### 2. "Procedural": Known Rules, Unequal Outcomes, Judgment, and Two-Part Forgiveness

Turn now to the second word of Principle 5: **Justice is scoped *procedural* symmetry.**

Why **procedural**? What is a procedure?

A **procedure** is a clear, repeatable method of action and adjudication whose rules are **stated in advance, known to all participants before the contest or dispute begins, and applied step-by-step to the evidence of what a person actually did.**

#### Rule of Law Versus Rule by Man (and Why Referee Whistles Cannot Depend on the Jersey)

Imagine walking onto a basketball court where there is no rulebook written down before tip-off. Instead, the referee announces: *"Play hard! Whenever I blow my whistle, I will look at the scoreboard, check how I feel about each team's historical background, and award points to whichever team I think deserves to feel better at that moment."*

Within five minutes, the game disintegrates. Why? Because when rules are invented after the play or bent depending on which jersey the player is wearing, **skill, preparation, and honest play become pointless, and lobbying or intimidating the referee becomes the only game in town.**

In political and legal history, this is the bright dividing line between the **Rule of Law** (procedural justice) and **Rule by Man** (arbitrary decree by kings, commissars, mobs, or administrative bureaucrats):

* When **Solon** reformed the laws of **Athens** in 594 BCE, he had the statutes carved onto rotating wooden and stone tablets (*axones* and *kyrbeis*) set up in the public marketplace where every citizen could read them in advance, binding magistrate and poor farmer to the exact same procedure. Even the ancient Near Eastern proverb of **"the law of the Medes and Persians, which alters not"** captured a vital discovery of early statecraft: once a decree was entered into the public law, not even the monarch could whimsically rewrite it yesterday's way to favor a court favorite today.
* Contrast Solon's procedural Athens with **Lycurgus's Sparta**, which romantic collectivists from Rousseau to modern authoritarians have endlessly praised for its supposed "equality" and martial discipline. Look under the hood of Sparta's outcome-equality:

    1. First, the "equality" of the Spartiate warrior caste (*Homoioi*) rested entirely on the terroristic enslavement of a massive subject population—the **Helots**, who outnumbered Spartans roughly seven to one and worked the land under threat of death. Every year, the Spartan magistrates formally declared war on their own helot laborers so that young Spartans in the secret police (***Krypteia***) could prowl the countryside at night and murder any helot who looked too strong, intelligent, or industrious without incurring religious blood-guilt!
    2. Second, inside the Spartan state upbringing (*agoge*), how were Spartan boys trained? They were deliberately underfed and **commanded to steal food from farms and storehouses**—and if a boy was caught stealing, he was whipped brutally, **not because stealing was wrong, but because he had been clumsy enough to get caught!** Think about what that did to the moral wiring of a society: it taught from childhood that property and truth have no intrinsic status, and that only stealth, force, and state utility matter. Unsurprisingly, historical accounts repeatedly noted that whenever Spartan commanders went abroad away from home surveillance, they were famously susceptible to bribery; and when Thebes finally shattered the Spartan phalanx at the Battle of Leuctra in 371 BCE and freed the Messenian helots, Sparta shrank into a backwater village that left behind not a single work of science, philosophy, architecture, or engineering.

#### Why Fair Procedure Applied to Particular Humans Produces Unequal Outcomes

Connect Principle 5 directly back to **Principle 2** (*Humans are self-interested, limited, and particular*).

Because human beings are **particular**—differing in innate talent, physical stamina, conscientiousness, family preparation, and daily effort—what happens whenever you apply an **identical, scrupulously fair procedure** to a hundred particular individuals?

**You get unequal outcomes every single time.**

Watch this in a concrete classroom or licensing hall:

* A civil engineering professor hands out the exact same **Structural Mechanics Final Exam** to a hundred students. Every student had access to the same lectures, the same textbook, and the same two hours in a quiet room. The exams are graded blindly by student ID number so the professor cannot see any student's name, race, sex, or family background.
* Student A spent twelve hours a week all semester solving practice problems and has a strong aptitude for spatial math; he scores **98 out of 100**.
* Student B spent two hours a week studying and struggles with calculus; he scores **54 out of 100**.
* **Was the exam unjust because Student A and Student B received unequal scores?** No! The unequal outcome is the **truthful measurement** of different preparation and competence under an identical procedure.
* What happens if the university dean steps in and says, *"Unequal scores are proof of systemic injustice! We must eliminate advanced classes, throw out the exam, and hand both Student A and Student B the exact same Structural Engineer license"*? Two disasters happen at once: first, **you stole the fruit of Student A's honest labor** and taught the next class of students that studying is for fools; second, **you lied about reality** (violating Principle 1) and certified an incompetent engineer whose bridge will collapse on innocent drivers.

#### The Necessity of Judgment: Why "Non-Judgmentalism" Is Suicide

Notice that a procedure is useless unless human beings have the courage to render a **verdict** at the end of the procedure: *True or false. Pass or fail. Honest merchant or fraudster. Guilty or not guilty.*

In a decadent culture, you will constantly hear the slogan *"Who am I to judge? We must never judge anyone's choices or lifestyle!"*

Test that slogan against reality for ten seconds:

* Can a carpenter build a house that stands up in a gale if he refuses to **judge** between seasoned white oak and punky, dry-rotted pine?
* Can a young woman build a happy marriage and raise safe children if she refuses to **judge** between a suitor who is sober, truthful, and industrious and a suitor who is a violent, pathological liar?
* Can a town stay safe if juries refuse to **judge** between the shopkeeper who worked sixty hours a week and the burglar who smashed his window with a brick?

**Discriminating judgment—applying truthful standards to reality and acting on the verdict—is the immune system of human life.** When a body's immune system stops judging between healthy host cells and invading gangrene bacteria, the body does not become "more inclusive"; it rots and dies. What Principle 5 forbids is *arbitrary prejudice* (pre-judging a person by tribe or bribe before looking at the evidence); what Principle 5 commands is **accurate procedural judgment** based on conduct and reality.

#### Mercy Versus Permissiveness, and the Architecture of Two-Part Forgiveness

At this point, a thoughtful reader asks: *"If justice is strict procedural symmetry and truthful judgment, where do **mercy** and **forgiveness** fit in? Are we supposed to be merciless machines?"*

On the contrary: **only a society with clear procedural justice can practice real mercy and real forgiveness**, because without a standard of judgment, what people call "mercy" is actually destructive **permissiveness**.

Look closely at the difference between **Mercy** and **Permissiveness**:

* **Mercy** begins by telling the complete truth about the law and the wrong: *"The law against theft is good and stands unbroken. You stole fifty dollars from this shopkeeper, and under the statute you owe full restitution plus thirty days in jail. Because this is your first offense, you have confessed truthfully, you have paid back the fifty dollars from your own wages, and your father has vouched to supervise your work this summer, the court exercises **mercy** to suspend the jail sentence on condition of clean conduct."* Notice that genuine mercy **upholds the standard, requires ownership and restitution, protects the innocent victim, and is scoped to a repentant offender.**
* **Permissiveness (Counterfeit Mercy)**, by contrast, refuses to enforce the law or call the wrong a wrong. When a prosecutor or judge watches a chronic thief steal from a neighborhood grocery store for the twentieth time and releases him onto the street an hour later with zero restitution and zero penalty while lecturing the shopkeeper about "compassion for the marginalized," that judge has not shown mercy. **Permissiveness toward the unrepentant predator is cruelty toward every honest grocer, mother, and child on that street.**

The exact same structural clarity resolves one of the hardest personal questions in human life: **When someone has betrayed, cheated, or harmed you, what does it mean to forgive them?**

Confusing two separate scopes of forgiveness ruins countless lives—either trapping victims in lifelong bitterness, or guilting victims into letting an unrepentant abuser walk right back into their house to hurt them again. Clear your mind by separating **Two-Part Forgiveness**:

1. **Part 1: Personal Release of Bitterness (Unilateral — Inside Your Own Mind and Heart, Tier 1).**

    - This first part of forgiveness is **unilateral**: it does not require the offender's permission, apology, or presence.
    - What is it? It is the conscious decision inside your own soul to hand the offender over to lawful justice and reality, and to **evict rancor, obsessive hatred, and vengeful brooding from your own mind** so that the person who harmed you does not get to live rent-free in your head for the next thirty years and poison your marriage, your work, and your sleep.
    - You can—and should—perform Part 1 even if the offender is dead, in prison, or completely unrepentant, because Part 1 keeps *your own* vessel clean and functional.

2. **Part 2: Social Reconciliation and Restored Trust (Bilateral and Procedural — Between Two Persons in Tiers 2–4).**

    - The second part—restoring relationship, letting the person back into your house, lending him your car again, or making him treasurer of the company—is **never unilateral**. It is **strictly procedural and conditional upon the offender's real-world conduct**.
    - What is the procedure for restored trust?

        1. **Truth (Virtue 1):** The offender must stop lying, drop all excuses, and admit plainly what he did.
        2. **Ownership and Restitution (Virtue 2):** He must pay back what he stole or repair what he broke to the fullest extent possible.
        3. **Tested Change Over Time:** Trust is lost in buckets and rebuilt in drops. A brother who stole from your till does not get the safe combination back the afternoon he says "I'm sorry"; he earns back small scopes of trust over months and years of verified honesty.

    - When an offender genuinely walks those steps, a wise community builds him a **"Golden Bridge"** back into productive life—because if a society offers zero path back for a repentant person who has paid his debt, it forces every offender into permanent outlawry.
    - Conversely, if an offender refuses to admit the truth, blames you for his crime, and continues the behavior, **you can complete Part 1 (hold zero personal bitterness) while keeping your front door bolted shut against him under Part 2.** Releasing bitterness is charity; handing your wallet back to an unrepentant thief is folly.

### 3. "Symmetry": The Blindfolded Scale, Boundary Conditions, and the Two Counterfeits

Now examine the third word of Principle 5: **Justice is scoped procedural *symmetry*.**

What does **symmetry** mean in geometry and engineering? It means that if you swap the labels of two parties who occupy the **same position inside the same scope**, the procedure does not change by a single millimeter.

* If Rich Citizen A breaches a written contract with Poor Citizen B, the court applies Procedure $X$.
* If Poor Citizen B breaches the exact same written contract with Rich Citizen A, the court applies the **exact same Procedure $X$**.
* That is why the classical statue of Lady Justice wears a **blindfold**: not because she is blind to the facts and evidence of the case, but because she refuses to peek at the wealth, political party, skin color, or family connections of the litigants before weighing the evidence on her scale.

#### Two Crucial Boundary Conditions of Symmetry

Because symmetry operates inside a procedure, two boundary conditions govern how it handles actors who try to break the procedure itself:

1. **Citizens Inside the Law vs. Armed Enemies Outside the Law:**

    Domestic criminal procedure—search warrants, Miranda warnings, bail hearings, proof beyond a reasonable doubt before twelve jurors—is the right symmetrical procedure **within the scope of domestic civic peace**. When an armed foreign army invades your territory, or a terrorist cell is actively firing rockets at your cities, they are operating in the scope of **warfare**, not domestic tort law. Treating an active military enemy on the battlefield as if he were a domestic misdemeanor defendant is a fatal confusion of scope.

2. **Honest Error vs. Deliberate Fraud (Putting a Thumb on the Scale):**

    Inside a peaceful community, two very different kinds of harm occur:

    - **Honest Error / Negligence:** A driver glances at his mirror at the wrong second and dents your bumper, or a bookkeeper accidentally transposes two digits on an invoice and immediately corrects it when pointed out. Here, ordinary symmetrical civil restitution—paying to fix the bumper or correcting the invoice—restores the balance.
    - **Deliberate Fraud (Attacking the Procedure Itself):** A merchant secretly hollows out his brass scale weights and fills them with cork; a litigant forges a property deed or bribes a juror; an academic fabricates laboratory data; a voter casts ten fraudulent ballots.
    - Why must deliberate **fraud** be punished vastly more severely than an honest mistake of the same dollar amount? Because **an honest mistake happens *inside* the game, whereas fraud is an attack on the referee, the rulebook, and the measuring tape itself!** In a high-trust society, millions of daily transactions run fast and cheap because people can rely on weights, deeds, certificates, and oaths without hiring three armed guards and a forensic chemist for every loaf of bread. A fraudster parasitizes that shared trust and forces the entire society to slow down and pay a permanent security tax. Therefore, procedural symmetry requires that anyone caught deliberately putting a thumb on the scale faces severe, exemplary penalties—treble damages, forfeiture of license, and prison.

#### Refuting the Two Counterfeits of Symmetry

Whenever a civilization forgets that symmetry belongs to **procedure**, it falls into one of two deadly counterfeits: **Outcome Symmetry ("Equity")** or **Value Symmetry (Retaliatory Feuding)**. Let us expose both:

#### Counterfeit 1: Outcome Symmetry ("Equity") and the Equalizer Class

Modern ideological activists explicitly draw a contrast between *Equality* (which they define as equal procedural rules at the starting line) and ***Equity*** (which they define as **Outcome Symmetry**—guaranteeing that every person or group ends up at the exact same finish line regardless of performance).

Ask three simple engineering questions about Outcome Symmetry:

1. **How can you force unequal inputs to produce equal outputs?** Only by **destroying procedural symmetry**—that is, by rigging the rules in reverse! If Runner A runs the hundred-yard dash in ten seconds and Runner B runs it in fifteen seconds, the only way to make them cross the tape at the exact same instant is to strap fifty pounds of lead onto Runner A's ankles or move Runner B's starting blocks thirty-five yards down the track. **Outcome Symmetry requires Procedural Asymmetry.** You cannot have both.
2. **Where does Outcome Symmetry naturally converge?** Remember Principle 3: building excellence takes heroic skill and sweat, whereas failure and death require zero effort. You cannot pass a law that magically gives every student on earth the mathematical genius of Isaac Newton or the surgical hands of the world's best neurosurgeon. **You can never equalize human beings upward to the ceiling; you can only equalize them downward to the floor.** Put a pilot who passed the flight test and a pilot who failed the flight test into an ice storm under a mandate of "equal outcomes": you cannot decree that both planes land safely, **Wait—you can only guarantee equal outcomes by crashing the good plane into the mountain alongside the bad one!**
3. **Who holds the scale in an "Equity" regime?** This is the dirty political secret of every outcome-equalizing movement in history: if the state promises to continually adjust the rules, confiscate property, and reassign seats until every statistical group has identical outcomes, **someone has to be given permanent, unchecked discretionary power to rig the scale every morning.** That creates a permanent ruling caste—an **Equalizer Class** of commissars, administrators, and diversity bureaucrats—who enjoy total exemption from symmetry while lording over everyone else. As George Orwell observed on the wall of *Animal Farm*, in every regime of outcome-equality, the equalizers who hold the paintbrush always end up *"more equal than others."*

#### Counterfeit 2: Value Symmetry (Retaliatory Feuding and Status Pricing)

The older, pre-modern counterfeit of justice is **Value Symmetry**: the belief that justice consists in inflicting back an equivalent "value" of blood or status hurt, priced according to the rank or tribe of the parties.

Watch what happens in two historical forms of Value Symmetry:

* **The Unending Clan Feud Ledger:** In blood-feud societies (from medieval vendetta clans to highland feuds), Clan A accidentally kills a herdsman from Clan B. Clan B calculates that *their* dead man was worth two ordinary men of Clan A, so they ride over and kill two men of Clan A. Clan A, viewing its own sons through the natural magnifying glass of inner-tier love, sees the killing of two of its men as an unprovoked outrage worth four men of Clan B! **The feud ledger book never closes**, because human beings are biased accountants of their own pain. Only when a third-party magistrate steps in with a **scoped, uniform procedural law**—taking the sword of vengeance out of private clan hands—can the blood ledger finally close.
* **Status-Graded Retaliation (*Lex Talionis* and *Diyya* Schedules) vs. Uniform Statutory Punishment:** In many ancient codes, including parts of the Code of Hammurabi and classical tribal or sectarian blood-money (*diyya*) schedules, the penalty for killing or injuring a person explicitly varied by the victim's caste, sex, or religion: knocking out the tooth of a nobleman brought a harsh penalty, while knocking out the tooth of a commoner or non-believer cost a few silver coins.
* Contrast that status-pricing with **uniform statutory punishment** under procedural symmetry—even when that punishment is severe and physical. Consider **Singapore's judicial caning** for violent robbery, vandalism, or extortion: the statute is written in advance in plain English; it applies identically to a wealthy foreign banker's son and a local dockworker convicted of the same offense; it is administered under strict medical supervision; and—unlike Western prisons that lock a young offender in a cage with hardened gang members for five years at massive taxpayer expense while his family falls apart—it delivers an unforgettable, immediate physical deterrent in ten minutes and sends the humbled offender back to work and support his household the next week. Severity is not injustice; **arbitrary asymmetry is injustice.**

### 4. Human Rights Under the Five Principles: Scoped Constitutional Liberties Versus Modern "Human Rights" Ideology

Now that we understand all three words of Principle 5—**scoped**, **procedural**, **symmetry**—we can cut straight through the confusion surrounding one of the most overused phrases of the modern age: **"Human Rights."**

Why does the phrase "rights" sometimes name the bedrock liberties of a free, high-trust republic, and at other times operate as an ideological battering ram that paralyzes police, bankrupts treasuries, and dissolves national borders?

Because two completely different things are hiding inside the same word: **Classical Constitutional Rights** (which obey Principle 5) and **Modern Universal "Human Rights" Ideology** (which systematically violates Principles 1, 3, 4, and 5). Let us separate them cleanly:

#### 1. What Classical Constitutional Rights Actually Are: Scoped, Negative Procedural Symmetries

When the English Bill of Rights (1689), the American Declaration of Independence (1776), and the United States Constitution and Bill of Rights (1787–1791) spoke of unalienable rights—such as life, liberty, property, freedom of speech and conscience, the right to keep and bear arms, and trial by jury—look at what those rights actually were in engineering practice:

* **They were *Scoped* to a Specific Polity:** The American Bill of Rights was written as the supreme procedural rulebook of the **United States of America**, enforced by American courts, defended by American soldiers, and maintained by the tax-paying, law-abiding citizens of that republic.
* **They were *Negative Procedural Boundaries* Against Arbitrary State Power:** Look at the grammar of the First, Second, Fourth, and Fifth Amendments: *"Congress shall make no law..."* *"shall not be infringed..."* *"shall not be violated..."* *"nor be deprived of life, liberty, or property, without due process of law."*

    - Notice what a negative procedural right costs the public treasury in scarce material goods: **zero dollars.** For the magistrate to refrain from gagging your speech, seizing your rifle, or stealing your farm without a jury trial does not require a carpenter to build you a free house or a farmer to hand you free wheat; it simply requires the state's officers to obey the same symmetrical boundary toward every citizen!

* **They were Welded to Civic Upkeep and Ownership (Virtue 2):** In the classical republic, the citizen whose property and speech were protected by the court was the exact same citizen who stood in the militia muster with his own rifle to defend the town, sat in the jury box to enforce the law against thieves, and supported his own household without begging from his neighbors.

#### 2. The Five Structural Flaws of Modern "Human Rights" Ideology

Contrast that classical constitutional architecture with the post-1945 **Universal "Human Rights" Ideology** promoted by United Nations committees, transnational NGOs, and activist courts. When tested against the Five Principles of Reality, modern human-rights ideology fails on five critical counts:

1. **Unscoped Universalism (Violating Principle 4 and Principle 5):**

    A real right cannot float in outer space; it exists in reality **only inside a defined jurisdiction (*scope*) where citizens pay the cost of enforcing it.** Modern human-rights ideology erases the word *scoped*. It declares that any of the eight billion human beings on earth—simply by existing biologically—possesses an immediate "human right" to walk across your national border uninvited, move into the **Winter Storehouse** (Section 1 above) that your parents and grandparents stocked, and demand housing, schooling, medical care, legal representation, and political power on the exact same terms as the citizens who built the storehouse. That is a direct assault on **Principle 4 (Tiered Obligation)** and **Principle 5 (Scope)**: an unscoped right for eight billion strangers is a sentence of liquidation for the host nation.

2. **Confusing Negative Procedural Symmetry With Positive Claims on Builders' Labor (Violating Principle 3 and Virtue 2):**

    Look at the ever-expanding laundry list of modern UN "human rights": the "human right" to a furnished dwelling, to guaranteed income, to university education, to paid leisure, to medical surgery, and to high-speed internet.

    Stop and ask the engineer's question from **Principle 3 (*Goodness must include human life continuing, and that life kept and built*)**: **Where do houses, grain, antibiotics, and fiber-optic cables come from?**

    They do not grow on trees or fall out of the sky when a diplomat in Geneva signs a parchment! Every house must be framed by a carpenter's calloused hands; every loaf of bread must be harvested by a farmer; every surgery must be performed by a trained surgeon.

    Therefore, **the instant you declare that a built material commodity is a "universal human right" owed to people regardless of whether they work or contribute, you have declared a right to conscript the labor and property of the builders!** One man's "positive right" to receive a free loaf of bread is, by mathematical necessity, a government gun pointed at the baker forcing him to bake for nothing.

3. **Rights Divorced From Responsibilities and Upkeep (Violating Virtue 2):**

    Recall the authoritative specification of **Virtue 2 (*Take ownership, which is responsibility*)**: civilization ***"cannot accept blaming others for one's own failures, demanding rights and privileges without carrying the corresponding upkeep."***

    Modern human-rights talk trains millions of people to speak only a language of **grievance and demand**—*"What are my rights? What am I owed?"*—while treating the mention of duties, law-abiding conduct, sobriety, family support, and civic upkeep as an "oppressive burden." A bridge where everyone claims the right to drive thirty-ton trucks across the span while nobody accepts the duty to bolt the girders or paint the steel falls into the river.

4. **Petitionary Grammar in the Face of Real Enemies (Violating Principle 1 and Principle 4):**

    Remember the Greek and Armenian shopkeepers in 1915 and 1942, and the Moriori on the beaches of the Chatham Islands in 1835 (Chapter 8). When an armed predator or a hostile regime comes to destroy you, waving a booklet of "Universal Human Rights" in his face has the exact same physical stopping power as waving a paper napkin at a charging grizzly bear. **Rights are not magic shields in physical reality; they exist only where armed, organized, virtuous human beings maintain the perimeter that enforces them.**

5. **Asymmetric Weaponization to Protect Predators Over Builders (Violating Principle 5):**

    Look at how "Human Rights Courts" and activist commissions actually operate in Western cities today:

    - When a twenty-time violent burglar or foreign gang member terrorizes a working-class street, the human-rights apparatus mobilizes ten lawyers to argue that locking him in prison or deporting him back to his home country would violate *his* "human rights" to comfort and family life.
    - Where were the "human rights" of the seventy-year-old grandmother he mugged on the pavement, or the shopkeeper whose life's work he burned down? Nowhere in the tribunal's brief!
    - Whenever "human rights" becomes a one-way shield that immunizes predators, rioters, and border-violators from consequences while tying the hands of honest citizens, police, and magistrates, it has become an anti-civilizational weapon (**Chapter 11**).

### 5. International Law: Why Scope Runs Out at the Border and Military Might Is the Ultimate Arbiter

Closely tied to the illusion of unscoped "human rights" is a second grand illusion that confuses domestic politics with global reality: the belief in **"International Law."**

Whenever a foreign dictator invades a neighbor, seizes shipping lanes, or builds covert nuclear weapons, commentators on television solemnly announce: *"That action is a violation of International Law! The international community must refer this to the International Court!"*—speaking as though the planet Earth were a big version of Ohio or Surrey, complete with a town courthouse, a police chief, and a patrol car on the way to handcuff the invader.

Let us state the engineering truth about the international arena without a shred of decoration:

> **Between sovereign nations, the scope of procedural symmetry runs out. There is no such thing as "international law" in the strong sense in which domestic law is law.**

Why? Trace the exact mechanics of **Principle 5 (*Justice is scoped procedural symmetry*)** and **Principle 4 (*Enemies are real*)**:

1. **Why Domestic Law Is Real Law (Inside the Polity Scope):**

    Why does a contract law, a property deed, or a murder statute work inside a functioning sovereign nation?

    Because inside that national perimeter, all citizens stand beneath **one shared sovereign authority** that possesses a **monopoly on legitimate physical enforcement**: a legislature that writes the statutes, courts that adjudicate disputes under a single procedure, and—standing right behind the judge's gavel—**armed bailiffs, police officers, sheriffs, and soldiers who will physically kick down the door, confiscate the bank account, and lock the recalcitrant lawbreaker in an iron cage if he defies the court's judgment.** Without the sheriff and the cage, a domestic statute is just a polite suggestion.

2. **Why "International Law" Is Not Law (Outside the Polity Scope):**

    Now step outside the national border onto the international plane between sovereign states. Look around:

    - Is there a global constitution to which China, Russia, Iran, the United States, and Singapore have surrendered their sovereignty? **No.**
    - Is there a global legislature above nations? **No.**
    - Is there a global court with its own independent army and police force? **No.**
    - **Is there a global sheriff who can drive up to Beijing, Moscow, Tehran, or Washington in a squad car and arrest a sovereign head of state or seize an army corps?** **Never.**

3. **What Treaties and "International Law" Actually Are in Reality:**

    What people loosely call "international law" is actually a collection of **bilateral and multilateral treaties, customs, and coordination protocols** (governing things like maritime shipping lanes, radio frequencies, postal delivery, diplomatic pouches, and trade tariffs).

    And how long does any international treaty bind a sovereign state in physical reality?

    **A treaty binds only so long as one of two physical conditions holds true:**

    - **Condition A (Aligned Self-Interest — Principle 2):** Both sovereign parties continue to find it convenient and profitable to keep the agreement (for example, both countries want their commercial airliners to land safely at each other's airports without colliding on the radio frequency); **or**
    - **Condition B (Credible Force — Principle 4):** A stronger military power stands ready and willing to make violating the treaty **catastrophically expensive** in blood, treasure, and regime survival for the violator.

    Look at historical reality whenever both Condition A and Condition B disappear:

    - In **1928**, almost every major power on earth—including Germany, Japan, Italy, the Soviet Union, France, Britain, and the United States—solemnly signed the **Kellogg-Briand Pact**, officially outlawing war forever under "international law." Eleven years later, World War II killed seventy million people.
    - In **1994**, Ukraine signed the **Budapest Memorandum**, giving up the third-largest nuclear weapons arsenal on earth in exchange for solemn written security assurances on paper from Russia, the United States, and Britain guaranteeing Ukraine's existing borders. When Ukraine gave up the physical weapons (**Condition B**) and kept the paper treaty, what was the paper worth when Russian tanks rolled across the border in 2014 and 2022? Less than the ink it was printed with.

    **The only real international law in the ultimate sense is military might**—backed by the economic industrial base, demographic vitality, and national will that build and sustain military might. Even the historical eras that looked most peaceful and "law-governed" across the oceans—such as the Roman Mediterranean (*Pax Romana*), nineteenth-century maritime trade under the British Royal Navy (*Pax Britannica*), or the post-1945 global shipping order under the United States Navy—looked law-governed **only because a dominant military power was underwriting the sea lanes with steel hulls and loaded guns.** Mistake the pleasant quiet created by the battleship for the permanent disappearance of pirates, and the moment you scrap the battleship, the pirates own the sea.

4. **The Asymmetric Trap of Supranational Tribunals (UN Bodies, ICJ, ICC):**

    Worse still, watch what happens when a law-abiding constitutional republic pretends that United Nations committees, the International Court of Justice (ICJ), or the International Criminal Court (ICC) are a genuine **Principle 5 courtroom**:

    - Look at who sits on the bench and casts the votes in United Nations bodies: totalitarian police states, theocracies, and aggressive dictatorships that jail every domestic dissident, recognize zero procedural symmetry at home, and laugh out loud whenever a UN resolution criticizes their own conduct.
    - Yet because Western constitutional democracies have been trained by their own domestic legal culture to revere anything carrying the label "Court" or "Law," **predatory regimes systematically weaponize supranational tribunals as Asymmetric Lawfare against free nations**—issuing politicized warrants and resolutions to paralyze a democratic state from defending its borders or crushing terrorists who hide behind civilian human shields, while the terrorist cell and its authoritarian state sponsors obey zero rules whatsoever!
    - **The Engineering Rule for Sovereign States:** Deal honestly in reciprocal treaties where verification works; never outsource your constitution, your courts, or your self-defense to supranational committees of tyrants; and never forget that beyond your national border, **scope has ended, enemies are real, and your nation's survival rests on your own industrial competence, trusted allies with shared interests, and armed strength.**
